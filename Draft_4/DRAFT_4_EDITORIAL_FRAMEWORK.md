## The Scalpel & Hose Process (Per Chapter)

### Step 1 — SCALPEL (Cut)

1. Adverbs: kill every one that doesn't earn its place
2. Passive voice: rewrite in active
3. Telling-after-showing: if you just showed it, cut the explanation
4. Over-explanation: cut repeated themes across multiple paragraphs
5. Purple prose: ground it or kill it
6. Throat-clearing: cut warmup paragraphs

### Step 2 — HOSE (Add)

1. Sensory place details (3+ senses per scene)
2. Character-specific dialogue voice
3. Money sentences (4–6 per chapter)
4. Physical grounding during emotional moments
5. Grace's backstory signals
6. Dan's guitar playing background
7. Dr. Marsh's tragic mirror

### Step 3 — WEAVE IN (From Research)

- The Neighborhoods reference (Maxanne 2009)
- Store 24 as period detail
- "Crash the silence" (Maxanne, once)
- The Rat as sacred ground
- "Landslide" as Patty's key
- Lennon's Springsteen warning (Maxanne, once)
- Phish/Mike Gordon (Maxanne, one subtle line)

### Step 4 — ENFORCE

- POV discipline (no head-hopping)
- Metaphor lanes (no crossing)
- Greek structure (felt, never named)
- The Honor Test: Does this honor Nick? Does this reach Jackie?

## Editorial Notes Format

Each chapter gets a companion file in `EDITORIAL_NOTES/` with:

- **Before score** (1–10, honest)
- **Scalpel log**: What was cut and why
- **Hose log**: What was added and why
- **After score** (1–10, honest)
- **Money sentences**: The lines readers will underline
- **Remaining concerns**: What still needs work
