## ACT I: SETUP - Chapters 1-8

### Chapter 1: The Last Voice of Boston Rock
**Frame: 2024, WBCN Studio**
- Maxanne begins final 4-hour broadcast
- WBCN dies at midnight after 56 years
- Introduces Nick & Jackie story
- Plays 93-second demo from 1975
- Themes: Endings, nostalgia, class division, authentic vs. commercial music
- **Emotional beat:** Melancholic, reflective, hint of bitterness

### Chapter 2: The Garage That Held More Than Cars
**Flashback: March 1979, 3 weeks before Battle of Bands**
- Maxanne drives to Dutton Road (working-class side)
- Meets Grace Johnson (Nick's mother, Friendly's uniform)
- Enters garage, meets Nick + 3 Lincoln boys band
- Dan Johnson's tools still on pegboard (father died 1977)
- Nick plays, Maxanne is moved
- Offers to record demo at WBCN
- **Emotional beat:** Discovery of raw talent, class struggle visible in details

### Chapter 3: The Gilded Cage
**Flashback: March 1979, same week as Ch 2**
- Dr. Richard Marsh calls Maxanne about Jackie's recital
- Maxanne visits Marsh house (Concord Road, upper-class)
- Recital for prep school parents, champagne, Steinway piano
- Jackie plays classical pieces flawlessly but mechanically
- Jackie breaks from program, plays "Under Pressure" (Queen/Bowie)
- Dr. Marsh furious, audience scandalized
- Maxanne gives Jackie her card
- **Emotional beat:** Suffocation in privilege, rebellion, recognition
