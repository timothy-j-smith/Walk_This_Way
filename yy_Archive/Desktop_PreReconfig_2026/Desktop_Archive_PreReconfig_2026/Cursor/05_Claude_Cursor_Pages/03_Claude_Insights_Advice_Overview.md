## 💡 **CREATIVE PROBLEM SOLVING**

### **Writer's Block Solutions:**
- "I'm stuck on this scene - what are 5 different approaches I could take?"
- "This character feels flat - what hidden depths could they have?"
- "This plot feels predictable - what unexpected twists could I add?"
- "This dialogue is boring - what conflict could make it interesting?"
- "This chapter feels unnecessary - what purpose could it serve?"

### **Alternative Thinking Techniques:**
- "What if I approached this from a completely different angle?"
- "What would happen if I reversed this situation?"
- "How would a different genre handle this scene?"
- "What if this character had the opposite personality?"
- "What if the setting was completely different?"

### **Creative Exercises:**
- "Generate 10 random story prompts and pick the most interesting"
- "What if this story took place in a different time period?"
- "How would this story change if told from a different POV?"
- "What if the protagonist and antagonist switched roles?"
- "What if this story had a completely different ending?"

## 📊 **QUALITY ASSESSMENT**

### **Story Quality Questions:**
- "What are the strengths and weaknesses of this story?"
- "How does this compare to successful books in the genre?"
- "What elements need improvement for professional quality?"
- "Is this story ready for publication or needs more work?"
- "What would make this story more compelling?"

### **Character Quality Assessment:**
- "Are these characters fully developed and believable?"
- "Do the characters have clear motivations and goals?"
- "Are the character arcs satisfying and complete?"
- "Do the characters feel unique and memorable?"
- "Are the relationships between characters realistic?"

### **Plot Quality Analysis:**
- "Is the plot structure sound and well-paced?"
- "Are there any plot holes or inconsistencies?"
- "Does the story have sufficient conflict and tension?"
- "Are the plot twists surprising but believable?"
- "Does the ending satisfy the story's promises?"

## 🎯 **EFFECTIVE CLAUDE INTERACTIONS**

### **Best Practices:**
- **Be specific** - Ask detailed, focused questions
- **Provide context** - Give relevant background information
- **Ask follow-ups** - Dig deeper into interesting responses
- **Be open-minded** - Consider alternative perspectives
- **Take notes** - Document valuable insights

### **Question Framing:**
- **Start broad** - Ask general questions first
- **Narrow down** - Focus on specific aspects
- **Explore alternatives** - Ask "what if" questions
- **Seek examples** - Request concrete illustrations
- **Test ideas** - Validate concepts with Claude

### **Response Processing:**
- **Read carefully** - Don't skim responses
- **Consider implications** - Think about broader applications
- **Ask for clarification** - Don't assume understanding
- **Synthesize insights** - Combine multiple responses
- **Apply practically** - Use insights in your writing

---

## 🚨 **COMMON MISTAKES**

### **Ineffective Questions:**
- **Too vague** - "Help me with my story"
- **Too broad** - "How do I write a novel?"
- **No context** - Asking without background
- **Leading questions** - Only seeking validation
- **One-shot questions** - Not following up

### **Poor Response Processing:**
- **Not reading fully** - Skimming responses
- **Ignoring advice** - Asking but not applying
- **Seeking validation only** - Not open to criticism
- **Not following up** - Missing deeper insights
- **Not taking notes** - Forgetting valuable advice
