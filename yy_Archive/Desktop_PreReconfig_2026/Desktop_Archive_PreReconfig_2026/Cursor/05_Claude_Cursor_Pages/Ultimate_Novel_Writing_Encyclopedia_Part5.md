## CHAPTER 21: PUBLISHING PATH DECISIONS

### P01: PUBLISHING PATH DECISION - STRATEGIC CHOICE

The Publishing Path Decision tool helps you choose between traditional publishing, self-publishing, or hybrid approaches based on your goals, timeline, and resources.

#### TRADITIONAL VS SELF-PUBLISHING ANALYSIS

**Traditional Publishing Advantages:**
- Industry validation and credibility
- Professional editing and design
- Distribution to bookstores
- Marketing support (varies by publisher)
- Advance payment
- Prestige and recognition

**Traditional Publishing Disadvantages:**
- Long timeline (1-3 years)
- Loss of creative control
- Lower royalty rates (5-15%)
- Difficult to get accepted
- Limited marketing support
- Rights issues

**Self-Publishing Advantages:**
- Complete creative control
- Higher royalty rates (35-70%)
- Faster time to market (3-6 months)
- Direct reader relationship
- Keep all rights
- Flexible pricing and marketing

**Self-Publishing Disadvantages:**
- All costs and work on you
- No industry validation
- Limited bookstore distribution
- Marketing responsibility
- Quality control challenges
- Stigma (decreasing)

#### DECISION MATRIX

**Choose Traditional If:**
- [ ] You want industry validation
- [ ] You have 1-3 years to wait
- [ ] You want professional support
- [ ] You're willing to give up control
- [ ] You have a strong platform already
- [ ] You write in a commercial genre

**Choose Self-Publishing If:**
- [ ] You want creative control
- [ ] You want to publish quickly
- [ ] You have marketing skills
- [ ] You want higher royalties
- [ ] You write in niche genres
- [ ] You're comfortable with business aspects

**Choose Hybrid If:**
- [ ] You want both approaches
- [ ] You have multiple books
- [ ] You want to test markets
- [ ] You have different goals per book
- [ ] You want to learn both systems

#### TIMELINE EXPECTATIONS

**Traditional Publishing:**
- Query agents: 3-6 months
- Agent representation: 6-12 months
- Book deal: 6-18 months
- Publication: 12-24 months
- **Total: 2-4 years**

**Self-Publishing:**
- Manuscript completion: 0-6 months
- Editing and design: 1-3 months
- Platform setup: 1-2 weeks
- Publication: 1-2 weeks
- **Total: 3-9 months**

## CHAPTER 22: AGENT QUERY & COMP TITLE SYSTEMS

### P03: COMP TITLE RESEARCH - MARKET POSITIONING

#### SELECTION CRITERIA

**Strong Comp Titles:**
- Published within last 3-5 years
- Similar genre and tone
- Comparable target audience
- Similar themes or setting
- Successful but not blockbusters
- You've actually read them

**Avoid These:**
- Classics (too old)
- Blockbusters (too big)
- Books you haven't read
- Books too similar (plagiarism concerns)
- Books that flopped
- Books by same author

#### ANALYSIS TEMPLATE

**Comp Title Analysis:**
```
Title: [Book Title]
Author: [Author Name]
Publication Year: [Year]
Genre: [Primary/Secondary]
Target Audience: [Age/Demographic]
Similar Elements: [What makes it comparable]
Key Differences: [How your book differs]
Why It Works: [Why this comparison helps]
```

#### POSITIONING STATEMENT

**Formula:**
"[Your Book Title] meets [Comp Title 1] meets [Comp Title 2]"

**Example:**
"The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo meets Gone Girl in a small town setting"

**Requirements:**
- 2-3 comp titles maximum
- Shows genre understanding
- Highlights unique elements
- Appeals to target audience
- Easy to understand

### P06: PITCH TOOLKIT - MULTIPLE FORMATS

#### ELEVATOR PITCH (30 seconds)
**Formula:**
"[Character] must [goal] before [stakes] when [obstacle]"

**Example:**
"A Portland journalist must expose her editor's murder cover-up before she becomes the next victim when she discovers three dead sources in six months."

#### TWITTER PITCH (280 characters)
**Requirements:**
- Include genre hashtag
- Character + conflict + stakes
- Compelling hook
- Under character limit

**Example:**
"Journalist Sarah discovers her editor is covering up murders. Three dead sources in six months. She's next unless she exposes the truth. #Thriller #MSWL"

#### CONFERENCE PITCH (10 minutes)
**Structure:**
1. Hook (30 seconds)
2. Character and conflict (2 minutes)
3. Plot development (4 minutes)
4. Climax and resolution (2 minutes)
5. Why you wrote it (1 minute)
6. Questions (30 seconds)

#### BACK COVER COPY (250 words)
**Elements:**
- Compelling opening
- Character and conflict
- Stakes and obstacles
- Comp titles
- Author bio
- Call to action

#### PRICING STRATEGY

**Ebook Pricing:**
- Debut novel: $2.99-$4.99
- Established author: $4.99-$9.99
- Series starter: $0.99-$2.99
- Box sets: $9.99-$19.99

**Print Pricing:**
- Calculate cost + 30% margin
- Research comparable books
- Test different price points
- Consider promotional pricing

#### DISTRIBUTION CHANNELS

**Wide Distribution:**
- Amazon (KDP)
- Barnes & Noble (Nook)
- Apple Books
- Kobo
- Google Play
- IngramSpark (print)

**Exclusive Distribution:**
- Amazon KDP Select
- Higher royalties
- Marketing tools
- Limited to Amazon

#### ROYALTY CALCULATIONS

**Amazon KDP:**
- 35% royalty: $0.35-$2.10
- 70% royalty: $2.10-$6.30
- Print: 60% of list price

**IngramSpark:**
- Ebook: 40% of list price
- Print: 45% of list price
- Distribution fees apply
