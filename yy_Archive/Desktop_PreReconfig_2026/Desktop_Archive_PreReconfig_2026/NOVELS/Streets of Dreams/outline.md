# Project Outline: The Last Football Match

## Chapter Structure
1. **Chapter 1: The Art of the Street** - Introduction to Youssef and street football culture
2. **Chapter 2: The Feline Academy** - Learning from the cats (metaphor for street skills)
3. **Chapter 3: The Electronic Window** - Discovery of video analysis through iPhone
4. **Chapter 4: The Twelfth Birthday** - Family celebration and anticipation
5. **Chapter 5: The Journey to the Coast** - First trip to Ain Diab beach
6. **Chapter 6: The Concrete Colosseum** - The challenge with academy players
7. **Chapter 7: Preparation for War** - Training and preparation week
8. **Chapter 8: The Beautiful Game** - The match begins, academy dominance
9. **Chapter 9: The Halftime Revelation** - Street team's tactical awakening
10. **Chapter 10: The Magic Cat's Moment** - Youssef's legendary goal
11. **Chapter 11: Heroes Return** - Victory celebration and recognition
12. **Epilogue: The Call** - Six months later, national team invitation

## Themes
- **Class and Access**: Street vs. academy represents economic barriers
- **Natural vs. Institutional Learning**: Cats and streets teach differently than coaches
- **Community vs. Individualism**: Success through collective effort
- **Dreams and Determination**: Underdog narrative with cultural specificity

## Key Metaphors
- **Cats as Teachers**: Each cat represents different football skills
- **Shadows as Opponents**: Youssef's imaginative training method
- **Concrete vs. Artificial Grass**: Symbolic of class differences

## Cultural Elements
- **Morocco 2022 World Cup**: Historical backdrop and inspiration
- **Medina Life**: Authentic street culture and family dynamics
- **Arabic Terms**: Contextualized cultural references
- **Religious Integration**: Call to prayer, family values
