## 🎯 **SPECIFIC INSTRUCTIONS FOR AI**

### **When Writing Prose:**
1. **Start with sensory detail** - what does the character see, hear, feel?
2. **Show emotion through action** - how does their body respond?
3. **Use specific, concrete details** - brand names, exact sounds, precise descriptions
4. **Create rhythm through sentence variation** - short punchy, long flowing
5. **Build subtext** - let meaning emerge through implication

### **When Writing Dialogue:**
1. **Make it authentic to 1979** - no modern slang or references
2. **Reveal character** - how they speak shows who they are
3. **Avoid exposition** - let information emerge naturally
4. **Use subtext** - what they don't say is as important as what they do

### **When Writing Action:**
1. **Use short, punchy sentences** for intensity
2. **Focus on visceral details** - physical sensations
3. **Build tension through rhythm** - vary sentence length
4. **Show consequences** - how actions affect the body

### **When Writing Reflection:**
1. **Allow longer, flowing sentences** for depth
2. **Use poetic imagery** to create atmosphere
3. **Connect to universal themes** - love, loss, hope, fear
4. **Build to emotional crescendos** through careful pacing

## 🚫 **WHAT TO AVOID**

- **Purple prose** - overly flowery or forced language
- **Anachronisms** - anything that didn't exist in 1979
- **Generic descriptions** - use specific, concrete details
- **Telling emotions** - show through action and sensory detail
- **Repetitive sentence structure** - vary length and rhythm
- **Expository dialogue** - let information emerge naturally
- **Inconsistent voice** - maintain character authenticity
