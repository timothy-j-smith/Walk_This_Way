### 2. **THEMATIC DEPTH**
Your exploration of American class dynamics through teenage friendship is:
- **Subtle** (shows rather than tells)

- **Resonant** (connects personal tragedy to systemic forces)

The novel's central insight—that authentic human connection is destroyed not by personal failings but by larger social machinery—is both timely and timeless.

**ACT II: BATTLE** - The competition becomes community examination of class assumptions

The frame device (Maxanne's final broadcast) provides natural pacing and emotional architecture.

### 4. **CHARACTER AUTHENTICITY**
Nick Johnson and Jackie Marsh feel **real**:
- Their friendship isn't destroyed by personal failings
- Their conflict emerges from forces larger than individual choice
- Their tragedy is both specific (Sudbury 1979) and universal (American class warfare)
