## 🚫 **WHAT'S HIDDEN (No Distractions):**
- ❌ **No red squiggly lines** under misspelled words
- ❌ **No popup suggestions** while typing
- ❌ **No status bar messages** about spelling
- ❌ **No automatic corrections** while writing
- ❌ **No interruptions** to your creative flow

## ✅ **WHAT'S AVAILABLE (When YOU Need It):**

### **Method 1: Right-Click Context Menu**
1. **Right-click on any misspelled word**
2. **See spelling suggestions** in the context menu
3. **Click to replace** or **add to dictionary**
4. **Perfect for**: Quick fixes while writing

### **Method 2: Command Palette**
1. **Press**: `Cmd+Shift+P` (Mac) or `Ctrl+Shift+P` (Windows)
2. **Type**: "spell" or "check spelling"
3. **Select**: "Spell: Check Document" or "Spell: Check Selection"
4. **Perfect for**: Checking entire chapters

### **Method 3: Keyboard Shortcut**
1. **Select a word** or **place cursor on a word**
2. **Press**: `Cmd+.` (Mac) or `Ctrl+.` (Windows)
3. **See quick suggestions** for that word
4. **Perfect for**: On-the-spot corrections

### **Method 4: AI Assistant (Composer)**
1. **Open Composer** (split screen)
2. **Ask**: "Check the spelling in this paragraph: [paste text]"
3. **Get AI-powered spelling and grammar help**
4. **Perfect for**: Complex editing and suggestions
