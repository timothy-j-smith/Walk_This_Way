# CHARACTER BIBLE - Walk This Way

## Main Characters

### Maxanne Sartori
**Age (2024):** 73 years old  
**Role:** WBCN radio DJ, narrator of the story  
**Background:** Been with WBCN since early 1970s, discovered Aerosmith in 1973  
**Voice/Personality:** Nostalgic, wise, slightly bitter, authentic Boston voice  
**Key Traits:** 
- Radio personality who values authenticity over commercialism
- Witnessed the transformation of Boston music scene
- Understanding of class divisions and their impact on music
**Arc:** Telling the story of Nick and Jackie on WBCN's final broadcast

---

### Nick Johnson
**Age (1979):** 17 years old  
**Age (1975, middle school):** 13 years old 
**Favorite Band: Aerosmith !!  
**Background:** Working-class kid from Dutton Road (wrong side of Hop Brook)  
**Family:** 
- Father: Dan Johnson (died in construction accident, November 14, 1977)
- Mother: Grace Johnson (works at Friendly's)
**Voice/Personality:** Working-class, passionate about music, raw talent  
**Musical Style:** Raw, emotional, Aerosmith-influenced rock  
**Key Traits:**
- Plays pawn shop guitar held together with electrical tape
- Natural rock voice, know as that "guitar guy" around town
- Lives in house where mortgage is always late
- Father's tools still hang in the garage where he practices
**Arc:** [To be developed - Battle of the Bands, conflict with Jackie]

---

### Jackie Marsh
**Age (1979):** 17 years old  
**Age (1975, middle school):** 13 years old 
**Favorite Band: Queen !!  
**Background:** Upper-class kid from Concord Road  
**Family:**
- Father: Dr. Richard Marsh (Boston University Psychiatrist)
- Mother: Eileen Marsh (elegant, carefully maintained)
**Voice/Personality:** Educated, conflicted about privilege, talented musician  
**Musical Style:** Classically trained, Queen-influenced, theatrical  
**Key Traits:**
- Voice trained by expensive teachers
- Piano prodigy, accepted to Juilliard pre-college program
- Struggles with parental expectations, wants to play Rock music
- Natural ability to blend classical training with rock and roll
**Arc:** [To be developed - friendship with Nick, Camp Winnipesaukee, Battle of the Bands]

## Supporting Characters

### Grace Johnson (Nick's mother)
**Role:** Working mother, widow  
**Background:** Lost husband Dan in 1977, works doubles at Friendly's  
**Key Traits:** Resilient, never complains, wears Friendly's uniform, name tag reads GRACE  
**Relationship to story:** Represents working-class struggle, supports Nick's music despite financial pressure

---

### Dr. Richard Marsh (Jackie's father)
**Role:** Father, professor  
**Background:** Boston University Psychiatrist 
**Key Traits:** Academic, upper middle class wanting to be upper class, concerned about Jackie's "social trajectory"  
**Relationship to story:** Represents upper-class assumptions and limitations

---

### Eileen Marsh (Jackie's mother)
**Role:** Mother, society figure  
**Key Traits:** Beautiful, carefully maintained, boarding school charm, moves like a dancer  
**Relationship to story:** Embodies upper-class aesthetics and expectations

---

### Dan Johnson (Nick's father - deceased)
**Role:** Father (died before main story), ghost presence  
**Background:** Construction worker, crane operator, died when crane cable snapped (November 14, 1977)  
**Key Traits:** Honest worker, organized (tools still hang in garage), represented working-class dignity  
**Relationship to story:** His death shapes Nick's worldview, his garage becomes Nick's temple
