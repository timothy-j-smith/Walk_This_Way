### 1. **OPENING PARAGRAPH** (Lines 4-8)
**Current Version:**
```

A garage where the sound punched you in the chest before you even opened the door.
```

**ISSUE:** Strong start, but the rhythm feels slightly flat in the second half. The short sentences after "But she'd heard rumors" feel disconnected.

**OPTION 1: More Musical Flow**
```
Maxanne Sartori drove up to the Johnson house in her powder-blue Karmann Ghia, the car looking ridiculous navigating the potholes that the town somehow never fixed on the working-class side of Hop Brook. The vehicle bottomed out twice, sending sparks dancing across the asphalt like tiny fireworks. She'd heard rumors about this place—a kid with a voice that could break your heart from three blocks away, a garage where the sound punched you in the chest before you even opened the door. The kind of rumors that made radio scouts like her drive to suburbs they'd otherwise never visit.
```
**WHY IT WORKS:** Connects the fragments into flowing prose while adding motivation for why she's there.

**OPTION 2: More Atmospheric**

**WHY IT WORKS:** Moves immediately into sensory experience and lets her hear the music sooner, building anticipation.

**OPTION 3: Keep Original Rhythm, Just Connect Better**
