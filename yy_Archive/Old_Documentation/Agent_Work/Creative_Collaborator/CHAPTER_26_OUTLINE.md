# CHAPTER 26 OUTLINE: THE BATTLE BEGINS

---

## OVERVIEW

**Chapter Title:** The Battle Begins
**Date:** May 15th, 1979 (Battle of the Bands night)
**Word Count Target:** 150-200 words
**Core Purpose:** Set the stage for the Battle of the Bands, introduce the judges, show the opening acts, build tension toward the main performances.

---

## STRUCTURED BEATS

### **BEAT 1: THE SETUP (50 words)**
- **Location:** Lincoln-Sudbury Regional High School auditorium
- **Time:** 7:00 PM, May 15th, 1979
- **POV:** Third Person Limited (focused on the crowd)
- **Characters Present:** 800 people in the auditorium, judges at the table
- **Purpose:** Establish the setting, the crowd, the tension
- **Key Elements:**
  - **Setting:** Packed auditorium, rented lighting, backstage green room
  - **Judges:** Maxanne Sartori (WBCN), Carter Alan (WBCN), Bob Walker (Strawberries Records)
  - **MC:** Chuck Morrison (WBCN morning drive)
  - **The Prize:** $500 and 100 hours of studio time
- **Ending:** Chuck announces the order of performance

### **BEAT 2: THE OPENING ACTS (100 words)**
- **Location:** Stage, Lincoln-Sudbury auditorium
- **Time:** 7:15-7:50 PM
- **POV:** Third Person Limited (focused on the crowd's reaction)
- **Characters Present:** The Jesters, Detroit Rock City, The Dead Heads, Talking Heads Talking
- **Purpose:** Show the opening acts, build tension, establish what's at stake
- **Key Elements:**
  - **The Jesters:** Freshmen doing Cars covers, technically competent but emotionally hollow
  - **Detroit Rock City:** KISS tribute, makeup running, platform boots, fake fire-breathing
  - **The Dead Heads:** Grateful Dead tribute, "Dark Star" goes too long, crowd gets restless
  - **Talking Heads Talking:** Boston Latin, new wave, crowd confused but respectful
- **Ending:** "The real competition was about to begin"

### **BEAT 3: THE TRANSITION (50 words)**
- **Location:** Backstage, Lincoln-Sudbury auditorium
- **Time:** 7:50-8:05 PM
- **POV:** Third Person Limited (focused on the three main bands)
- **Characters Present:** Nick, Jackie, Patty, their bandmates
- **Purpose:** Build tension toward the main performances
- **Key Elements:**
  - **Backstage:** Three bands preparing, nervous energy
  - **The Stakes:** This isn't just a competition anymore
  - **The Tension:** Everyone knows what's coming
- **Ending:** "The Battle of the Bands was about to become personal"
