**Scene: Ephraim Curtis Middle School Cafeteria, September 1974**

**Setting:**

**The Meeting:**
- Jackie Marsh (12) sits alone, new kid
- Wears brown leather belt with GOLD "QUEEN" script buckle (like Olympic medal)
- Nick Johnson (12) enters scanning for seat
- Wiry, restless energy
- Black leather belt with CHROME "AEROSMITH" gothic buckle

- Their eyes meet - not recognition, but buckle recognition

**The Dialogue:** (Screenplay lines 35-86)
- Nick: "Queen? Seriously?"

- Nick showing his buckle: "Nothing. Just... Aerosmith's better."
- Jackie standing: "Better? Have you heard 'Bohemian Rhapsody'?"
- Nick: "Have you heard 'Dream On'?"
- Disco music continues (Bee Gees now)
- Jackie disgusted: "Can we agree that disco sucks?"
- Nick bonding: "Oh my God, finally someone who gets it!"
- Jackie: "Where's the artistry? Where's the emotion? What is a 'Bee Gee' anyway?"
- Nick sits down, extending hand: "I'm Nick. I think I saw you in Mr. Apfel's homeroom."
- Jackie: "Jackie. My dad just moved us here from Ithaca."
- Nick: "So... Queen versus Aerosmith. This is happening..."
- Jackie grinning: "Oh, it's happening."

**Ending:**
- "Staying Alive" plays, middle schoolers singing along
- Jackie and Nick shake their heads in disgust
- First alliance formed: mutual hatred of disco
- Beginning of friendship built on musical debate
