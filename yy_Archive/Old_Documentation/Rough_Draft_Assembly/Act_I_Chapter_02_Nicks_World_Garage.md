**House Description:**
"The Johnson house squatted like it was tired: peeling paint the color of old mustard, sagging gutters that had given up pretending to channel water properly, front steps that listed to port like a ship taking on water slowly, gracefully, inevitably. **Grace:**
"She studied me—this Boston radio personality in bell-bottoms and oversized sunglasses, standing on her cracked front step like some visitor from a planet where people had money for clothes that didn't come from Jordan Marsh clearance racks. 'You're that lady from WBCN. The one who broke Aerosmith.' 'Guilty as charged.' 'Nick's in the garage.' She paused, and I saw something flicker across her face—hope, maybe, or fear that hope was dangerous, expensive, something they couldn't afford to budget for. 'Don't fill his head with dreams we can't afford.'"

**Garage Sound:**
"But from the detached garage came something alive—distorted power chords that made my radio-trained ears perk up like a dog hearing its master's key in the lock."

**Maxanne's Reflection (2009):**
"Back in the booth, 2024, I watch my engineer scroll through his phone. He has no idea what a garage means to rock and roll. He grew up with GarageBand, thinking music could be manufactured in a bedroom with the right plugins. He never felt the sacred geometry of four walls, concrete floor, and kids who had nothing to lose."
