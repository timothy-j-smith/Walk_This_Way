## 🎨 WHAT SEQUENTIAL THINKING MCP CHECKS

### **1. Character Consistency**
- **Checks:** Character traits, preferences, relationships
- **Against:** Story Bible (`Story_Bible/character-bible.md`)
- **Example:** "Nick loved disco" → Warning: Nick loves Aerosmith!

### **2. Timeline Accuracy**
- **Checks:** Dates, ages, events, chronology
- **Against:** Story Bible timeline, Continuity Tracker
- **Example:** "Dan died in 1977" → Verify: Story Bible says 1976

### **3. Purple Prose Detection**
- **Checks:** Draft 2 style violations
- **Against:** Draft 2 Style Guide (grounded, sensory-specific)
- **Example:** "His heart thundered" → Warning: Purple prose

### **4. Plot Continuity**
- **Checks:** Plot threads, character arcs, story logic
- **Against:** Previous chapters, blueprint
- **Example:** Chapter reference doesn't match previous chapter

### **5. Date Consistency**
- **Checks:** Age calculations, timeline events, references
- **Against:** Character Bible, Timeline
- **Example:** Nick's age in 1979 matches birth year?
