**Purpose:** Bridge between innocent friendship (Act I) and high school division (Act II)

---

## SCENES TO WRITE:

**Scene 1: Return from Camp**
- Both boys back in Sudbury
- Try to maintain the camp magic
- Geography reasserts itself
- Harder to get together (parents busy, distance)

**Scene 2: First Day of High School (Fall 1975)**
- Lincoln-Sudbury Regional High School
- Freshman orientation
- Social groups forming immediately
- Nick gravitates toward working-class kids
- Jackie gravitates toward college-bound kids
- They see each other in hallways but don't share classes

**Scene 3: Cafeteria - First Lunch**
- Geography of cafeteria mirrors town geography
- "The Ramp" (elevated section) for college-bound kids
- Jackie sits up there (his natural place)
- Nick sits at ground level with working-class kids
- They make eye contact across the divide
- Wave but don't sit together
- Other kids noticing: "Why you friends with him?"

**Scene 4: First Pressure**
- Nick's friends: "You still hanging with that rich kid?"
- Jackie's friends: "That Johnson boy is kind of rough, isn't he?"
- Both boys defend the friendship but feel the strain
- First time they hear the questions that will grow louder

**Scene 5: Attempted Garage Session**
- Jackie comes to Nick's house after school
- They try to play together
- The magic is still there in the music
- But conversation is harder
- Different experiences of high school
- Different concerns, different futures being mapped

**Scene 6: Letter from Patty**
- Both receive letters from Patty in Concord
- She asks if they're still playing together
- Both write back separately (not together like they would have at camp)
- Lies of omission - not mentioning the growing distance

**Scene 7: Chapter Ends**
- Maxanne narrating: "High school is where America teaches its children to divide themselves"
- "They were still friends. But the cracks were forming"
- "And then, in Spring of 1976, everything would shatter"
- Foreshadowing Dan's death
