## CORE STORY (UNCHANGING)

**Central Theme**: Two talented teenage musicians from opposite sides of a class divide—best friends who create magic together—are torn apart by America's class machinery, parental expectations, and death. Their friendship becomes rivalry, their music becomes weapon, and what could have been harmony becomes division.

**The Question**: Can music bridge the class divide, or does America's social machinery always win?

**The Answer**: The labyrinth wins. But someone has to document what existed before it was destroyed. Someone has to say "The harmony still exists" even when it doesn't anymore.

## PROSE STYLE TARGETS (NEW FOR DRAFT 2)

**ELIMINATE**:
- Purple prose (flowery descriptions, overwrought metaphors)
- Over-explaining emotions that should be shown
- Repetitive phrases used as crutches
- Abstract philosophizing instead of concrete moments
- Telling when showing would work better

**EMBRACE** (Richard Russo + Dennis Lehane + Stephen King):
- **Russo**: Warmth, generosity, seeing dignity in working-class lives
- **Lehane**: Gritty Boston authenticity, neighborhoods as characters
- **King**: Raw emotion, direct language, horror of ordinary moments
- Concrete sensory details (smell of garage, sound of cafeteria)
- Dialogue that reveals character without explanation
- Silences that say more than words
- Restraint: trust the reader, trust the story

**VOICE**: Gritty Boston 1970s rock and roll, some humor, authentic but not nostalgic, direct but not blunt, emotional but not sentimental
