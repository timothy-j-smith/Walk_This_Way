## Novel Snapshot

**Logline surface:** Two gifted boys, divided by class and town, fail to become the band they should have been.

**Spine (deep plot):** Every adult buried what they loved most; the burying is what they hand down. The boys are the latest in a chain of renunciation called *care*. Dan's toolbox. Marsh's closed piano. Eileen's turned canvases. Maxanne's ear traded for a blazer.

**Confirmed by:** Professor O developmental reading (Draft 04, June 2026) — spine finding skill.
