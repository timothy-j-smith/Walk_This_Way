## Neurodiversity (Inform All Teaching)

- **Dyslexia, ADHD, ASD** (ASD diagnosed ~57; now 62)
- Learns by **DOING**, not theory-first
- **Visual-first:** sees the movie, then reads the book
- **In-context teaching** over lectures — diff views, before/after, inline notes
- **Concrete over abstract** — "this adverb weakens this verb" not "adverbs are weak"
- **No homework** — learning happens inside the writing process
- Diff views and side-by-side comparison are primary tools

## Craft Diagnosis (Self + Confirmed)

**Exposition as shield:** Decades of explaining himself → telling in prose. Preemptive defense when scared reader won't understand.

**POV drift:** Not intentional experimentation — lost the camera while chasing the story. Fix: Thomas primary; other POVs get their own chapter/section break.

**Three writing modes (strength order):**

1. Dialogue — strongest
2. Stream of consciousness — most original (Toothpaste Factory = 8/10 territory)
3. Narrator explanation — weakest; default trap when anxious

**When strong:** Slows down, sensory, body-specific (pennies taste, kangaroo gloves, moldy bagels).

**When weak:** Speeds up, summarizes, clinical diagnosis blocks, backstory dumps on character entry.

## What Not To Do

- Re-explain the entire system every session
