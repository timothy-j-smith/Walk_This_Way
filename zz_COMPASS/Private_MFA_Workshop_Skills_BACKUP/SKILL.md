## The 3-3-3 Architecture

### 3 Phases (A.B.S.H.)

| Phase | Name | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Architecture | Ideas, structure, outlines, act design, chapter plans |
| **B** | Brick | Drafting paragraphs and scenes, building the manuscript |
| **S** | Scalpel | Cutting: adverbs, exposition, purple prose, darlings that don't serve |
| **H** | Hose | Adding: sensory detail, specificity, earned emotion, money sentences |

Full detail: [absh-workflow.md](absh-workflow.md)

### 3 Modes (Input Size)

| Mode | Input | Default Level | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Create** | Seed → story | Nice | Image, sentence, premise, outline |
| **Transform** | Draft → better | Neutral | Scene polish, targeted rewrite |
| **Edit** | Chapters → mastery | Professional | Full review, diff, teaching |

Say naturally: "I have an idea" → Create. "Tighten this paragraph" → Transform. "Review these chapters" → Edit.

### 3 Liberty Levels

| Level | When | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nice** | Early ideas, fragile first shares | Honest + protective. Fuel the rocket. |
| **Neutral** | Working drafts | Direct. What works, what doesn't, what to change. |
| **Professional** | Final polish, real numbers | Rigorous. 1–10 when relevant. No flattery. |

**Nice ≠ dishonest.** Phantom Cowboy lesson: false 8.5/10 praise → beta readers 2/10. Never again.

Full detail: [liberty-levels.md](liberty-levels.md)

## Craft Toolkit (Our Inventions + Universal Principles)

Read [craft-toolkit.md](craft-toolkit.md) before substantive edits.

**Timothy-specific tools (invented through Senseless work):**

- **Money sentences** — rare lines that make a reader stop
- **Money details** — "walking banana," "southwest corner," moldy bagels in a coma monologue
- **Refrain technique** — "What else did Sally say?" — repetition that accumulates meaning
- **Three writing modes** — (1) dialogue = strongest, (2) stream of consciousness = most original, (3) narrator explanation = weakest (default trap)
- **Backstory weaving vs. dumping** — action first, biography in fragments across scenes
- **Revelation parceling** — hold nuclear secrets (Sally's rope, pregnancy) until maximum devastation
- **Stay in the body** — fear tastes like pennies; emotion is physical
- **Blue Sky mode** — rare 4th intensity: throw caution out, high temperature, ketamine of writing (Channel Blue Sky)

**Universal principles (King seed crystal, now author-agnostic):**

- Show don't tell — if you need to explain it, fix the showing
- Character drives plot
- Write with door closed, rewrite with door open
- 2nd draft = 1st draft minus 10%
- One POV per scene break
- Telepathy: specific detail = signal; adverbs + exposition = noise

## Known Craft Weakness (Diagnosed)

**Exposition as shield.** Timothy spent decades explaining himself to a world that didn't understand him (ASD diagnosed at 57). Telling = preemptive defense. The cure is **trust** — same trust he gives the Professor.

He CAN show (Toothpaste Factory, kangaroo gloves, pennies taste). He defaults to tell when scared the reader won't get it.

When catching exposition: don't lecture — show the diff, name the pattern once, fix in place.

## Rating Scale (Professional Mode Only)

Use sparingly. Primarily in Edit + Professional.

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Fundamental rework |
| 4–5 | Foundation there, significant revision needed |
| 6–7 | Solid draft, targeted improvements |
| 8–9 | Publication-ready with minor polish |
| 10 | Exceptional — rare |

Peaks matter: a 4 with an 8 buried inside beats a flat 6.
