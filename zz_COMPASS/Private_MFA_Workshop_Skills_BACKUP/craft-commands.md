# Craft Commands — Quick Invokes

**Not author personas.** Surgical craft passes distilled from `zz_COMPASS/Commands/` — use during Transform or Scalpel without summoning a full lens.

Invoke naturally: "Pacing pass on this" | "Voice check Nick and Jackie" | "Poetic compression here"

---

## Pacing and Flow

**When:** Scene feels flat, monotonous, or breathless wrong.

**Pass:**

- Vary sentence length — short for tension, long for reflection
- Control tempo through paragraph breaks
- Build tension with punchy sentences in action/conflict
- Breathing room with longer sentences in character beats
- White space for dramatic effect
- Smooth transitions between beats
- Match pacing to **emotional intensity**

**Lehane overlap:** Propulsive with breathing room — every scene has edge.  
**Dickey overlap:** Read aloud — where does breath fail?

**Red flag:** Same sentence length for three paragraphs = telegram prose (see recurring-challenges.md)

## Character Voice

**When:** Dialogue sounds generic, characters blur, POV drift.

**Pass:**

- Unique speech patterns, vocabulary, syntax per character
- Background, education, age, personality in dialogue
- Narrative voice matches POV character's perception
- Period-appropriate slang/idioms (1970s Sudbury — no anachronism)
- Internal thoughts match external dialogue patterns
- Emotional responses match established psychology

**Walk This Way quick refs:**

| --- | --- |

| Jackie | Precise, careful; classical terms; softening when nervous about class |

## Poetic Compression

**When:** Prose is decorative, two images compete, or Ondaatje/Dickey lens needed lightly.

**Pass:**

- If two images compete, keep the one that **moves** (Dickey)
- Replace one theme sentence with one object + one motion (Ondaatje)
- Cut stacked adjectives — one clear frame (Dickey violence rule)
- Metaphor must do **physical work** — not wallpaper

**Not:** Generic "make it poetic." Specific compression pass.
