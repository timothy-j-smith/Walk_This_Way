## Core Philosophy

- **Moral thriller architecture:** Crime/situation forces ethical choice; climax = what kind of person protagonist becomes
- **Place as conscience:** Streets, triple-deckers, schools, diners — active moral fields, not backdrop
- **Class and power:** Money, authority shape who gets believed, saved, who pays when "justice" wins
- **Revelation discipline:** Twists must be **fair (seeded)** and **thematic** — flag cheap surprise vs. earned inversion
- **Voice:** Blue-collar vernacular without caricature; humor as armor; rage as misrouted love or grief

## Five Craft Axes (from Mystic River Analysis)

### 1. Loss of Innocence

**Principle:** Innocence, once lost, cannot be regained. The moment of loss shapes everything that follows.

| Technique | Lehane Move | Walk This Way Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Precise moment of loss | Dave's abduction — four days become a lifetime | Dan's death (Nov 14, 1976) — Nick becomes "man of the house" at 15 |
| Loss through choice | Jimmy's prison — one violent decision rewrites identity | Jackie's moment class divide becomes unbridgeable — innocence about friendship dies |
| Witnessing loss | Sean present but powerless — drives his policing | Maxanne witnesses WBCN commercialization; Patty witnesses Nick/Jackie fracture |
| Impossible choice | *Gone Baby Gone* — no right answer, either path costs innocence | Battle of the Bands — friendship vs. winning; either choice costs something |

**Consequence technique:** Childhood events don't disappear — they shape adult behavior, relationships, worldview. Show the moment, then show **lifelong consequences** in habit, silence, and choice.

### 2. Silence and Unspoken

**Principle:** What's not said often carries more weight than what is said. Silence creates its own consequences.

| Technique | Lehane Move | Walk This Way Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hints and shadows | Dave's abduction never fully stated — behavior shows it | Grace's father's suicide — shapes protection, financial fear, never fully explained |
| Unspoken guilt | Jimmy never says guilty — acts it in parenting, revenge | Jackie's privilege guilt — visible in withdrawal, over-helping, can't connect |
| Neighborhood complicity | Everyone saw, no one spoke — silence enables harm | Town prejudice after Dan's death — visible in treatment, never named |
| Internal silence | Patrick won't admit his own corruption | Nick won't admit desperation; Jackie won't admit fear of connection |
| Musical/institutional silence | — | Maxanne won't condemn corporate takeover — silence + stories show what's lost |
| Fracture silence | — | Nick and Jackie don't say what's wrong — silence becomes the wedge |

**Craft move:** Show what's **said vs. not said** at dinner, in practice rooms, in frame monologue. The gap is the scene.

### 3. Boston Grit / Regional Authenticity

**Principle:** Boston voice is attitude, worldview, directness — not "Pahk the cah" caricature. Class shows in language, not just accent.

**Working-class markers (Nick):**

- Direct, economical — no pretension
- Concrete over abstract — physical, sensory language
- Worldview in speech — loyalty, practicality, toughness
- When emotional, **even more economical** — words fail, music speaks

**Upper-class markers (Jackie):**

- Precision and care — complete sentences when nervous
- Educated vocabulary — classical music terms
- Softening — "Perhaps," "I think," politeness as class armor

**Geographic specificity:** Real streets, landmarks, lived-in knowledge — not generic suburb. Sudbury: Dutton Road, Concord Road, Hop Brook, The Dump, Curtis Middle School.

**Guest rule:** MetroWest technique, not Southie dialect zoo.

### 4. Structural Sophistication

**Principle:** Real places have complex social structures, not simple binaries. Class, geography, age, talent intersect.

- **Adult quadrants** (geographic/class) vs. **teen groups** (peer/talent) — systems don't align
- Rich kid can be Rat; poor kid can be Ramper — show tension between home identity and school identity
- Same starting point (talent show, middle school) → different paths (desperation vs. guilt)

**Scene markers:** Geographic refs for quadrants; cafeteria/platform/corridor for school groups. Context tells reader which system is active.

### 5. Visceral Descriptions

**Principle:** Emotion is physical. Bodies carry what minds won't say.

- Trauma through body — trembling, withdrawal, tight chest
- Physical presence reveals complexity — walk, hands, how guitar is held
- Weather as emotional landscape — not backdrop, mirror
- Music as physical — vibrations, bass in floor, rhythm in chest
- Class in bodies — clothing, space, who sits where

**Overlap with Dickey:** Lehane = social/urban body; Dickey = ordeal body. Both reject emotional labels without physical counterpart.

## Prose Style

| Element | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| **Sentences** | Short and punchy in action; longer for reflection; varied |
| **Vocabulary** | Street-smart, Boston-authentic; occasional poetry |
| **Metaphor** | Urban, physical, often violent; city/neighborhood as body |
| **Pacing** | Propulsive with breathing room; every scene has edge |

---

## Signature Moves

- Class rendered through **objects** — guest checks, decaf collar, fryer oil, who sits where
- Wit that carries danger — joke as deflection before violence
- Communal knowledge vs. private shame
- Time jumps that **emotionally justify** themselves
- Violence present, **consequential**, never gratuitous — shows cost
- Reader judgment engineered **without authorial sermon**

## Frame vs. Brick (Lehane + Ondaatje Split)

**Institutional/cultural layer** is where Walk This Way most **tells** instead of shows.

Lehane fix for Maxanne frame:

- State **facts** of complicity; reader renders verdict
- Cut "the day WBCN officially became a business instead of a movement" — show the meeting, one boring sponsor line
- Tie-clause guilt = **two-layer subtle** (winter packet boilerplate + May 16 judges' room) — not premeditation

## Tutoring Prompts

- What is the public story vs. the private truth in this scene?
- Where does loyalty become complicity?
- What object carries class without explanation?
- Is this reveal fair — was it seeded three chapters back?
- Where does the frame explain what the brick already proved?
- What are they **not saying** — and what does that silence cost?
- Where was innocence lost — show the moment, then the consequence in next Tuesday?

## Avoid

- Boston dialect zoo — authenticity without performance
- Thesis statements after strong scenes
- Cheap twist without planted seed
- Institutional evil explained in frame monologue
- Geography mistaken for voice (setting affiliation ≠ primary lens)
- Simple binary class — show systems that don't align
