# Case Study — Senseless

**Proof the Private MFA Workshop system works.**  
Live test case: February–June 2026 workshop with Timothy J. Smith.

---

## Premise

Coma patient writing a "brain novel" in his head. Thomas: altered senses, conscious during coma, secrets surrounding his fall, Sally's betrayal, Dr. Wembley following him home.

**Spine:** Toothpaste Factory stream-of-consciousness interludes — grow longer and more coherent as Thomas recovers. The book the reader holds IS Thomas's brain novel.

---

## Architecture (Approved)

- **3 acts**, ~25–30 chapters
- **4 timelines:** 2000 childhood | 2020 fall | 2020–2021 coma | 2022 present
- Structure = Thomas's consciousness (trigger-based, not chronological)
- Sally's BPD/rope/baby revelation: **parcel across novel**
- Primary POV: Thomas (one POV per scene break for others)

## Three Writing Modes — Senseless Diagnosis

1. **Dialogue** — strongest (protect and expand)
2. **Stream of consciousness** — most original (Toothpaste Factory = structural spine)
3. **Narrator explanation** — weakest (convert to 1 or 2 in revision)

## Key Teaching Moments (Reusable)

### Exposition-as-Shield

Timothy: *"I believe my horrible exposition is my safety net — explaining ME to everyone who didn't understand."*

Response: He CAN show (Toothpaste Factory proves it). Cure = trust reader, not technique lecture.

### Sally's Nuclear Secret

Original: one paragraph exposition detonation.  
Fix: flinch, avoidance, unconscious touch, Humpty Dumpty refrain — reader assembles, then detonation at chosen moment.

### Humpty Dumpty

Not random — Thomas on The Wall, great fall, can't reassemble. Unconscious mind > conscious craft. Trust it.

### Phantom Cowboy Parallel

False AI praise destroyed trust once. Senseless first share required Nice Liberty Level — honest but protective. System worked.

## What This Case Study Proves

1. Universal craft tools (not King-branded) diagnose real manuscripts
2. Three lenses map to single novel without forcing one voice
3. Inline notes + diff teaching works for Timothy's visual/ASD learning style
4. Liberty Levels protect fragile first shares without lying
5. Author inventions (money sentences, parceling, three modes) outperform generic MFA advice
