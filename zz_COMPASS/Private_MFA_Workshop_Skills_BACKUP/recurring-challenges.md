# Recurring Craft Challenges

**Universal patterns** — from MFA Master Document v2.1 + Walk This Way Pass 2 lessons. Not book-specific; apply across all novels.

Cross-reference: [craft-toolkit.md](craft-toolkit.md) | [absh-workflow.md](absh-workflow.md)

## The Explain-After-Write Pattern

**Symptom:** Strong declarative sentence, then a second sentence interpreting what the first already meant.

**Fix:** Cut the second. If the first isn't enough, make the first more specific — don't add explanation on top.

**Timothy root:** Exposition-as-shield (ASD masking parallel). Same cure as Senseless: **trust**.

---

## The Label Noun at Paragraph End

**Symptom:** Paragraph ends with a one-word or short label. *Panenka.* *Poetry.* *Trigonometry.*

**Diagnostic:** What does the labeled noun do that the preceding sentence doesn't already do?

**Fix:** If nothing — cut the label. The image already landed.

---

## Scalpel Without Hose → Telegram Prose

**Symptom:** Stacked subject-verb beats. *She saw X. She saw Y. He went inside. Nick went to his room.*

**Reads like:** Police report, summary, column — not King/Lehane/Ondaatje register.

**Fix (Hose pass):**

- Long sentence loads image or list
- Short sentence fires once (*Forty feet.* / *They sat.*)
- Fragments OK for **catalog** (cafeteria geography, clique texture)
- Fragments NOT OK for plot recap or emotional summary

**Rule:** Every brick/scalpel pass ends with **read-aloud**. Three lines sound like a column → merge before save.

**Walk This Way origin:** Ch 13 lesson, locked in `04_Prose_DNA.md` and `PARKING_LOT.md`.

## Leave the Wound Open

**Symptom:** Scalpel cut something false; writer fills gap with plain language to close it.

**Fix:** When scalpel cuts and hose does not flow — **leave the space open**. Do not fill with generic prose. The true line finds the cut later.

**Related:** Over-correcting precision into generality. *Specific over ornamental* — NOT *generic over ornamental*.

---

## The Hose Flowed — Writer Kept Writing

**Symptom:** Truth-line arrives; nervous system adds trailing simile, explanatory phrase, second image to make sure first one landed.

**Fix:** **Stop at the hose.** Reader does not need the confirmation clause.

**Diagnostic:** Name the moment the line should have stopped. Propose trim.

---

## Mistaking Discipline for Coldness

**Symptom:** Revision strips so much that prose feels flat or clinical.

**Truth:** **Specificity is warmer than ornament.** Cut decoration; keep sinew. Physical truth beats language reaching for feeling.

---

## POV-Voice Drift in Word Choice

**Symptom:** Ornamental adult vocabulary in teen POV or working-class interior.

**Fix:** Test every elevated word against character perception. Would this person think/say this word?

---

## POV Temporality Drift

**Symptom:** Mixing immediate present-tense consciousness with retrospective adult intrusion without licensing the move structurally.

**Timothy pattern:** Drifts rather than commits. Fix: pick camera + tense per scene; section break to shift.

**Walk This Way contract:** Maxanne = first present (2009). Brick = third past (1974–1979). No Maxanne gloss on brick meaning.

---

## Sports-Movie / Triumphal Arc Default

**Symptom:** Structure defaults to underdog victory, viral resurrection, clean catharsis.

**Flag when:** Ending "rescues" tragedy the book earned. Walk This Way: **no virality**, downbeat ending, "best band that never was" stays true.

---

## Flinching From the Deeper Book

**Symptom:** Structural revelation arrives; first instinct = "that's a different book" — protect current shape.

**Usually wrong.** Real revisions arrive **cool**, not hot. Spine finding skill helps recognize what's already there.

---

## The Staleness Floor

**Symptom:** After 30–50 reads of same passage, writer can no longer hear the prose.

**Normal.** Writer is not the right judge at staleness floor. **Professor + read-aloud + fresh eyes** required.

**Timothy:** Do not keep re-reading alone hoping to "hear it again." Hand it to workshop.

---

## Research Is Material, Not Script

**Symptom:** AI search engine or research doc includes narrative recommendations mixed with facts; recommendations get imported as plot decisions.

**Fix:** Research Triage skill. Facts → usable. Recommendations → return to writer. Professor does not import search engine's story ideas.

---

## Frame Tells Meaning (Walk This Way + Universal)

**Symptom:** First-person frame narrator tells the story AND tells what the story means. Pre-chews verdict for reader.

**Distinction:**

- ✅ Narrator tells the story (compression, gesture, withhold)
- ❌ Narrator tells you what the story means (thesis after image)

**Fix (Ondaatje lens):** Replace one theme sentence with one object and one motion. Trust the bird. Cut the gloss.

## Method-Level Breakthroughs (Carry Forward)

| Breakthrough | Application |
| --- | --- |
| Specificity is warmer than ornament | Hose pass: physical truth, not decorative language |
| Spine = what surface plot serves | Architecture; spine finding skill |
| Hose = truth after false is cut | Not brainstorm; arrives after scalpel |
| Crowd around work carries inheritance | Silent elders witnessing — generational history without naming |
| Staging the reading matters | Research before ms vs. after — choose deliberately |
