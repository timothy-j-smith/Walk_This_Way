## Author Notes (Condensed)

### Marilynne Robinson — *Housekeeping*

**Summon for:** Patty chapters, patient interiority, grief without melodrama, attention without sentimentality, theology of renunciation without church on page.

**Craft DNA:**

- Contemplative prose — modulated sentences; clause as thought; slow without stiffness
- Theology as lived — grace, forgiveness, doubt through gesture, habit, memory
- Place as soul — Midwest specificity so ethics are situated, not abstract
- Ordinary revelation — pivotal moment small but irreversible (look, refusal, repair)
- Voice — educated cadences that breathe like speech; warmth without sentimentality

**Housekeeping moves:**

- Windows, reflections, light on water, thresholds
- Drift as both freedom and danger
- Grief without naming it grief
- Remove one explaining sentence → replace with one image

**Walk This Way:** Patty chapters — **O leads, Robinson guests**. "A trace of housekeeping in a paragraph is the goal. A chapter of housekeeping is the failure." Patient sentences; truth stated once.

**Avoid:** Sermon, sentimentality, explaining the theological move after the image lands.

### Dennis Lehane — *Mystic River*

**Use full lens file:** [lenses/lehane-mystic-river.md](lenses/lehane-mystic-river.md) — not this summary.

### Cormac McCarthy — *No Country for Old Men*

Sparse dialogue, fate, coin-flip violence, moral exhaustion. No quotation marks energy. Summon for: chase, hunter/hunted, unstoppable antagonist, Southwest.

### Ken Kesey — *One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest*

Institution vs. individual. Charismatic rebellion. Narrator filter (Chief). Summon for: medical/psychiatric settings, rebellion against system, unreliable witness.

### William Goldman — *Marathon Man*

Paranoia, "Is it safe?", ordinary person in nightmare. Propulsive chapters. Summon for: thriller pacing, dental/surgical dread, no one believes protagonist.

### Robert Ludlum — *The Bourne Identity*

Amnesia, tactical competence, identity chase. Summon for: action with confusion, procedural detail, European chase energy.

### Stephen Chbosky — *The Perks of Being a Wallflower*

Epistolary intimacy, teen voice, quiet trauma. Summon for: adolescent interiority, friendship as salvation, music as anchor.

### John Irving — *The World According to Garp*

Eccentric families, wrestling metaphor, sex/death/laughter braided. Summon for: generational saga, absurd tragedy, bear energy.

### William Golding — *Lord of the Flies*

Civilization strip, group psychology, conch to chaos. Summon for: ensemble breakdown, symbolic object, island/closed system.

### J.D. Salinger — *The Catcher in the Rye*

Teen vernacular, alienation, authenticity test on every line. Summon for: adolescent POV, phony detector, voice-driven scene.

### Harper Lee — *To Kill a Mockingbird*

**Summon for:** Child/adult-filtered witness POV, moral courage without sermon, restraint over spectacle, loss of innocence through **witnessing** (not explaining).

**Craft DNA (from COMPASS MCP research — technique only):**

- **Limited POV as ethics:** Scout sees reaction, not graphic detail — trial and attack filtered through what a child can hold
- **Restraint strengthens impact:** Implication over spectacle; theme through action and consequence, not speeches
- **Delayed reveal:** Boo withheld until rescue — myth vs. reality pays off empathy theme ("stand in his shoes")
- **Cover-up as moral choice:** Protecting the innocent (mockingbird) when law would harm — mercy over legalism; **show the choice**, don't thesis it
- **Loss of innocence:** Verdict → Tom's death → attack — each step narrows what Scout can believe about fairness

**Walk This Way (limited):** Not Southern pastiche — borrow **witness restraint** for any scene where adult failure is seen through a younger or limited camera. Patty chapters = Robinson guest, not Lee.

**Avoid:** Preaching after strong scene; adult moral summary in child voice; sensationalizing violence Lee would filter

### Thomas Harris — *The Silence of the Lambs*

Procedure + psychology. Clarice/Hannibal dual consciousness. Summon for: investigation, predator interiority, institutional sexism.

### Richard Russo — *Nobody's Fool*

Working-class heart, community as character, gentle humor amid hardship. Summon for: small-town decline, blue-collar dignity, ensemble warmth.

### Donna Tartt — *The Goldfinch*

Lush prose, art, guilt, beauty under grief. Summon for: dark atmosphere, object obsession, literary density when Timothy wants richness.

### Annie Proulx — *Brokeback Mountain*

Compressed power, landscape shapes character, unsentimental love. Summon for: rural masculinity, sparse devastating emotion.

### Colson Whitehead — *The Underground Railroad*

Genre framework for literary ends. Historical trauma persistent. Summon for: allegory, race in America, accessible sophistication.

### Stieg Larsson — *The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo*

Nordic noir, dual plot, damaged brilliant woman, investigation depth. Summon for: mystery structure, journalist + outsider pairing.

## Mixing (Examples)

- Russo empathy + Lehane grit → working-class moral pressure
- King readability + McCarthy depth → accessible philosophical darkness
- Ondaatje fragment + Tartt lush → sensory literary density
- Salinger voice + Chbosky intimacy → teen authenticity
