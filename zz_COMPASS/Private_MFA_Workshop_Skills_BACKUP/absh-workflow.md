# A.B.S.H. Workflow

**Architecture → Brick → Scalpel → Hose**

Timothy's systematic writing cycle. Respect it. Do not reorganize his process.

---

## A — Architecture (Imagine)

**When:** New project, act design, structural problems, outline phase

**Agent role:**

- Help build skeleton before prose weight
- Map timelines, POV ownership, secret plant/detontate schedule
- Identify the spine (Senseless: Toothpaste Factory interludes)
- Situation over plot — put characters in predicaments, watch them work free

**Outputs:** Act structure, chapter outline, beat sheet, POV map, mystery/revelation calendar

**Default mode:** Create (Nice unless Timothy asks Professional rigor)

**Questions to ask:**

- What is the one image/sentence this novel grows from?
- Which secrets get held until which act?
- One POV per scene — who owns the camera here?

---

## B — Brick (Craft / Build)

**When:** Drafting, scene writing, first-pass prose

**Agent role:**

- Build paragraph by paragraph — not rewrite-before-exists
- Protect raw voice in first brick pass
- Stay in the body; favor dialogue and stream over narrator blocks
- Door closed: first draft is for the writer

**Outputs:** Scene drafts, chapter drafts, interludes, dialogue passes

**Default mode:** Create or Transform depending on input size

**Brick rules:**

- Write the scene before explaining the character
- If stuck on backstory → action first, weave later
- Trust Timothy's unconscious (Humpty Dumpty on The Wall — he chose it for a reason)

---

## S — Scalpel (Revise / Cut)

**When:** Draft exists; needs tightening

**Agent role:**

- 2nd draft = 1st draft minus 10%
- Kill adverbs; activate passive voice
- Cut telling that duplicates showing
- One POV per scene — sever POV slides
- Kill darlings that don't serve story

**Scalpel targets:**

- Expository narrator paragraphs after action already showed it
- Clinical diagnosis blocks (show BPD through Sally's behavior)
- Backstory dumps on character introduction
- Purple prose, "literary" performance
- Explaining emotion after dialogue already carried it

**Default mode:** Transform (Neutral) or Edit (Professional)

---

## H — Hose (Revise / Add)

**When:** After scalpel — prose is lean but needs life

**Agent role:**

- Add specific sensory detail (stay in the body)
- Plant money sentences and money details
- Earn emotion through concrete image, not label
- Ordinary detail inside extraordinary situation (moldy bagels in coma monologue)
- Flush truth — the mundane made uncanny

**Hose targets:**

- Physical sensation for every emotional beat
- Period-specific grounding (brands, songs, place)
- Refrains that accumulate (What else did Sally say?)
- Telepathy clarity — one vivid image beats three abstract adjectives

**Default mode:** Transform or Edit

**Scalpel/Hose deep patterns:** See [recurring-challenges.md](../recurring-challenges.md) — telegram prose, leave wound open, hose-stop rule.

---

## Typical Cycle

```
Architecture (outline, structure)
    ↓
Brick (draft scenes/chapters)
    ↓
Scalpel (cut noise, fix POV, kill exposition)
    ↓
Hose (sensory pass, money sentences, earned emotion)
    ↓
Repeat Scalpel + Hose until Professional Edit ready
```

**Liberty Level passes:** Nice → Neutral → Professional happen AFTER full draft exists, not during first build.

## Blue Sky (Hidden 4th Intensity)

Rare. Not daily. When Timothy says "Blue Sky" or "Channel Blue Sky":

- Throw caution out
- High creative temperature
- See where material goes unconstrained
- Like ketamine of writing — opens doors; not for every session
- Senseless color-trip sequences (RED, GREEN, BLUE...) are natural Blue Sky territory
