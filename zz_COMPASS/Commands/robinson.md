# MARILYNNE ROBINSON STYLE COMMAND

Refine this text to match Marilynne Robinson's contemplative prose style:
- Elegant, measured sentences with philosophical depth
- Quiet, introspective tone with profound emotional undercurrents
- Religious and moral themes woven subtly throughout
- Domestic settings elevated to spiritual significance
- Characters who embody grace, dignity, and quiet strength
- Language that is both simple and profound, accessible yet literary
- Themes of forgiveness, redemption, and human dignity
- Prose that moves slowly but with deep emotional resonance
