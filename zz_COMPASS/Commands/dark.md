# DARK ATMOSPHERE COMMAND

Create dark, threatening, or ominous atmosphere:
- Ominous weather (storms, fog, darkness)
- Shadows, isolation, and confined spaces
- Unsettling sounds and environmental details
- Symbolic elements that suggest danger or foreboding
- Period-specific details that enhance the dark mood (1970s urban decay for your novel)
- Environmental details that reflect internal character anxiety or fear
- Use of lighting and shadow to create visual tension
- Atmospheric details that build toward conflict or revelation
