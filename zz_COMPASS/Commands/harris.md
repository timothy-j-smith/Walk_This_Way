# THOMAS HARRIS STYLE COMMAND

Intensify this text to match Thomas Harris's psychological thriller style:
- Clinical, precise prose that creates unease through restraint
- Psychological depth with focus on the criminal mind
- Forensic detail and procedural accuracy
- Building tension through what is left unsaid
- Characters who are both brilliant and deeply disturbed
- Atmospheric, often gothic settings with psychological weight
- Sophisticated, intelligent prose that doesn't condescend
- Themes of evil, justice, and the nature of humanity
