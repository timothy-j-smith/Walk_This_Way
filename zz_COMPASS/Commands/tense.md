# TENSE ATMOSPHERE COMMAND

Create tense, suspenseful, or anxious atmosphere:
- Confined spaces and environmental pressure
- Ticking clocks, deadlines, or time pressure
- Sensory details that create unease and anticipation
- Symbolic elements that suggest impending conflict or revelation
- Period-specific tension details (1970s political/cultural tension for your novel)
- Environmental details that reflect character stress or urgency
- Use of contrast between calm and chaos
- Atmospheric details that build suspense toward climactic moments
