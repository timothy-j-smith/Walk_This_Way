# TENSION AND SUSPENSE COMMAND

Increase dramatic tension and reader engagement:
- Build suspense through unanswered questions and delayed revelations
- Use foreshadowing and ominous details to create unease
- Control information flow - what the reader knows vs. what characters know
- Create conflict and obstacles that raise stakes
- Use short, sharp sentences for immediate tension
- Build to climactic moments with rising action
- Include psychological tension through character internal conflicts
- Maintain reader curiosity and investment in outcomes
