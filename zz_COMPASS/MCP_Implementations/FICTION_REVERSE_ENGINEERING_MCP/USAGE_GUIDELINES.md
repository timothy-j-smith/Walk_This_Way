# FICTION REVERSE ENGINEERING MCP - USAGE GUIDELINES
## When and How to Use This Tool Appropriately

**Created:** January 24, 2026  
**Purpose:** Ensure this MCP enhances creativity rather than limiting it

---

## ⚠️ CRITICAL PHILOSOPHY

### This MCP is a LEARNING TOOL, NOT A FORMULA

**DO:**
- ✅ Use to understand patterns in bestsellers
- ✅ Learn pacing techniques mathematically
- ✅ Compare your work objectively
- ✅ Extract insights for improvement
- ✅ Understand structural patterns

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Copy formulas blindly
- ❌ Force your writing into rigid patterns
- ❌ Replace creative intuition with formulas
- ❌ Use as a crutch for every decision
- ❌ Over-formulaize your unique voice

---

## 🎯 WHEN TO USE THIS MCP

### ✅ APPROPRIATE USES:

#### 1. **Learning Phase** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**When:** Studying bestsellers to understand what works
**Example:**
- "Reverse engineer 'Mystic River' to understand Lehane's pacing"
- "Extract dialogue patterns from 'Gone Baby Gone'"
- "Analyze chapter structure in 'Shutter Island'"

**Why:** Learn from masters without copying

---

#### 2. **Diagnostic Phase** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**When:** Something feels off but you can't identify why
**Example:**
- "Compare Chapter 4 pacing to 'Mystic River' formula"
- "Analyze why Act II feels slow"
- "Check if tension curve matches thriller patterns"

**Why:** Objective analysis reveals hidden issues

---

#### 3. **Revision Phase** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**When:** Polishing and refining after creative draft
**Example:**
- "Compare pacing to bestseller formula for improvements"
- "Analyze dialogue rhythm against successful thrillers"
- "Check chapter structure against proven patterns"

**Why:** Use data to refine, not create

---

#### 4. **Quality Assurance** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**When:** Final polish before publication
**Example:**
- "Verify pacing matches genre expectations"
- "Check tension curve is appropriate"
- "Ensure reveal frequency is optimal"

**Why:** Final check for professional quality

---

## ❌ WHEN NOT TO USE THIS MCP

### 1. **During Creative Drafting** ❌
**Why:** Formulas kill creativity during initial creation
**When:** Writing first draft, exploring ideas, creative flow
**Instead:** Use Structural Perfection Specialist (qualitative)

---

### 2. **For Every Decision** ❌
**Why:** Over-reliance on formulas creates formulaic writing
**When:** Making every plot decision, every scene choice
**Instead:** Use intuition first, then verify with this tool

---

### 3. **To Copy Bestsellers** ❌
**Why:** Copying formulas creates derivative work
**When:** Trying to replicate another author's success
**Instead:** Learn patterns, adapt to your voice

---

### 4. **Instead of Your Voice** ❌
**Why:** Your unique voice is your greatest asset
**When:** Formulas conflict with your authentic style
**Instead:** Trust your voice, use formulas as reference
