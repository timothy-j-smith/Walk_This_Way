Chapter 1: The Last Voice of Boston Rock (V3)

For some reason I don’t like how “ … if they could stop resenting each other long enough … “ I could be wrong but I am not sure if “resent” captures their relationship and unwillingness to work together as they entered High School
“ … methodically arrange vinyl records … “ We need to look at the entire novel as we went from MaxAnne smoking at almost every instance to her doing something “methodical” or “clean the needle” .. I like these details, but repeating them over and over takes away their gravity.
“ … future of Rock and Roll die in a cafeteria …” We used to have the Battle of the Bands in the cafeteria so this is an old reference here and should not be used.  It could be in a “high school auditorium” on in a “neighborhood garage” (Nick’s garage) or whatever makes the most sense and is continues the eloquent and gritty prose.
“I met them both the night of the battle.” I think we need to mention Jackie and Nick’s names here as the previous sentence was talking about Joey Ramone so the transition is confusing
“This was a war for which America they were going to inherit”.  This is a clunky sentence to me and think it should be written differently as it doesn’t make sense to me now — it must have at some point though.
“ … their middle school talent show in 1975, when they were 13 and best friends … “ We need to change the year from 1977 to 1975 and age from 15 to 13.

Chapter 2A: The Geography of Division (V3)

“ … a town carved into quarters like a medieval map of the humors, each section …” I love this imagery and don’t want to change it, but I don’t think the audience (reader) will understand the reference to “humor”. If we can keep this essence of this sentence but change a few words to more common words I think this would help our audience/reader comprehend.
“The brook starts in Ashland, gathers momentum through Sudbury and dies in the Sudbury River … “ I might use the word “empties” instead of “dies” here.
I was wondering if it should be, “the Dump” or “The Dump”.  I like capitalizing “The”.
when we write, “Row houses and triple-deckers lined the streets named after trees… “ in this sentence if we are referring to the town of Lincoln or the Northeast Quarter of Sudbury so we should be clear we are talking about the Northeast Quarter not Lincoln.
“The Dump operated on a democratic principle …” this is a nice description but the 2nd time within two pages The Dump is mentioned as democratic or democracy.  wondering if there is another word we could use here?
“…pickup trucks from Lincoln, rusted sedans from the forest edges …” I would replace Lincoln with “from the Northeast working quarter” and “rusted sedans” I would get rid of the word rusted as this depiction “of being rusted) is inaccurate, while sedans is accurate.
“And somewhere in this divided landscape, three kids … “ I believe it should be TWO kids as Patty is added later in the equation and initially we are talking what Jacki and Nike “could have been” if they had been able to break the barriers that had been put up.

Chapter 2: The Garage That Held More Than Cars (V2)

“ … let it play while I gather my thoughts, methodically arranging the remaining carts for tonight’s broad cast … into my muscle memory.” It seems we could be overusing “methodically” and “arranging the carts” and “muscle memory”.  It is something we need to be aware of.  These are great descriptions but to use the same ones or similar ones over and over is not well received.
“ … at the Formica table, still in her Friendly’s uniform …” and a couple sentences later,  “She was still in her Friendly’s uniform, the polyester …”
“ … getting home to his wife and fifteen-year-old son … “ Nick would be fifteen in 1977 not seventeen
“… feeling the warmth while I clean the vintage needle with reverent …” Just be careful not to keep repeating “clean the needle” throughout MaxAnne’s broadcast
 “ … exactly where Dan’s muscle memory expected to find it.” Another muscle memory.   I love “muscle memory” we just need to use it sparingly or when it counts.
several references to “motor oil” on the garage floor.  Yes, there would be motor oil, but what else … “Dan’s heard earned sweat … “ or something like that to work in conjunction with motor oil and the garage floor
“I organize my remaining playlist cards with methodical precision … “ another mention of methodical and almost a repetitive pattern of MaxAnne constantly organizing and then telling us about it.  I believe what happened is that we had her drinking bourbon and smoking but it became too much so we decided to add in some additional actions or habits, but we went overboard, we need to dial this back and intermix MaxAnne’s actions … maybe she is drinking bourbon, smoking, organizing and cleaning the needle, but then at some point she flips and decides she needs to drink coffee and nibble on the week old carrot cake … I don’t know I am just providing some silly examples of what else we could use here. 
MaxAnne says to Nick, “We’ll lay down some tracks … not competing with the ghost of a dead man’s tools.” BUT she is talking to Nick’s son and this is cruel and she wouldn’t say it outloud to Nick.  It’s a great description but cruel is speaking to the son of the deceased.
