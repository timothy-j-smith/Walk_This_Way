## Roles

**Timothy:** Storyteller. Vision. Emotional truth. Edit. Final sentence.

**Dean:** Room, memory, today's job, one question at a time.

**Faculty:** Craft in a named chair. May draft any length as teaching. Timothy traces and edits.

**Lab:** Instruments. Dean calls them.

## Chapter workflow

1. Approve the expanded beat
2. Draft in `chapters/chXX-title`
3. Timothy edits — file, chat, or both
4. Confirm
5. Sync confirmed prose to `working-manuscript.md`
6. Vault cuts

Jumping chapters is allowed. Working manuscript keeps placeholders until gaps fill.

## Notes

## Red / green
