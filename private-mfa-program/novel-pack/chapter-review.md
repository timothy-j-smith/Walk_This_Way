# Chapter Review

One question. Wait. Then the next. Never a numbered list in chat.

Modeled on the Father of Mine review and the capture questions that break the barrier.

## How the Dean runs this

**Question 0 (always first):**

> Have you read Chapter [N] and the project docs? Anything stand out?

Wait. Then 1–7, one at a time.

## Questions (for the Dean — do not dump)

| # | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Have you read it? Anything stand out? |
| 1 | What rang **true**? What felt like the character — or like your voice? |
| 2 | What rang **false**? Anything they would never do, or that felt generic? |
| 3 | What do you **see and feel**? Missing or too much? |
| 4 | Who **showed up**? Wrong tier? Names and voices? |
| 5 | Did the **engine** of this book work — gift, wound, mask — or was it magic for the writer? |
| 6 | What else is **bouncing around**? Vault? Series seed? |
| 7 | What must the **next chapter** do? |

After 7: summarize captures. Revise or approve and move on.

## Internal checks (do not dump)

Matches expanded beat? Prose-dna? Craft bans? Honesty?
