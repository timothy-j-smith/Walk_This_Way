# Idea Capture

New story only. One question. Wait. Spelling does not matter.

Save answers to a dated file. Then title, copy the rest of this pack, write `prose-dna.md`. Do not cut a book that does not exist.

## Questions (Dean asks one at a time)

1. What is the idea that is exciting you
2. Why does this excite you
3. What do you see and feel
4. Who or what is it about
5. What is the what-if
6. What else is bouncing around
7. What happens next

Optional, only if still flowing: anything else while you are in it.

Always offer skip, choose, or write-in.

## After capture

Organize later. Outline is a different job. Today's job after capture is usually: save, name the north star, pick faculty in `prose-dna.md`.
