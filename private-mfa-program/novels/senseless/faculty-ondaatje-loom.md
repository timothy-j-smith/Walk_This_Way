## What I read

You already wrote the English Patient’s situation without naming it.

A damaged man. A body that has become a map. Other people orbit him. One of them washed him when he could not move. One of them never came to the bed. One of them is a friend who has taken the fiancée. The past does not arrive in order. It arrives because sand feels like grain, because pennies sit on the tongue, because a pearl goes in the mouth.

The short story dumps. The novella-in-pieces still dumps: Jackie-O’s whole life on the trail, vasculitis as lecture, Sally’s rope and baby and BPD in one granite sit-down, Jaws and seals as research. Professor K saw that. He was right.

The Factory monologue is the book. Untouched. Correct.

That loom is a good teaching scaffold. It is not how this novel wants to be *written*.

## Where I differ from Professor K

K was right about the heart and wrong about the machinery.

**Keep:** Factory as spine. Do not open inside the coma. Do not throw away the beach. Parcel Sally. Protect the father phone call, the eyelid scene, Shake & Bake, moldy bagels, “I am the one,” skating’s last line.

**Drop:**

1. **Labeled interludes.** *The English Patient* does not say INTERLUDE. The desert walks into the villa because of a plum, a map, a name. Pennies, pearls, sand, hands in the mouth — those are already your doors. Use them. Do not file the coma in a marked box between chapters.

2. **The magic-trick ending.** “The book you are holding IS the brain novel.” That is clever architecture. It winks. This book should simply *be* that consciousness. If you explain the trick, you kill it.

3. **Four timelines announced.** You have three rooms that rhyme. That is enough.

4. **Spending the Factory as Part 3, then saving “Act 3: The Toothpaste Factory” as a title for later.** The Factory is not a third act you arrive at. It is the white room the other two rooms keep falling into. Your own note on Interlude 4 — how much to save — is the right worry. Answer: **never dump the whole Factory at once.** Let it occupy more of the page as he comes back. The last Factory stretch is the most coherent. That is recovery, not a finale title.

King already did his job in February: stay in the body, cut the dumps, do not touch the stream. Dickey can have The Wall when Thomas climbs. Harris can have Wembley when the gun is the point. This job is the loom. That is why I was called.

## The three rooms

Not beach / hike / factory / skating as four legs of a trip.

| Room | In the pages | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sand and water** | Nauset, Mirko, waves, below the turbulence | The world after. How to survive |
| **Stone and air** | Saddle, The Wall, granite, Humpty, the color trip | The wound’s geography. What Sally did lives here, unsaid |
| **White and mouth** | Factory, toothpaste, bagels, pearls, Wembley’s hands | The coma. The book being written where no one can hear it |

Skating is sand-and-water in winter. Ice is another floor under him. It is not a fourth room.

Jackie-O is already renamed (Melinda, Mindy-O, Jackie-O). That is identity as this book understands it. Do not explain the nickname on the trail. Let the real name surface once, late, like a map with the wrong country on it.

Sally’s power is that she **never sits in the white room**. Everyone else did. That absence is louder than BPD.

## How the novel moves (high level)

Not twenty-eight chapters. Seven movements. Each one is a stretch of pages, not a homework beat sheet.

### 1. The beach (you have this)

Thomas already awake. Elephants. Rashford’s dent in the sand. Mirko. The wave. “What else did Sally say?”

Do not start in the hospital. Professor K was right. I agree. We meet the burned man in the villa, not in the fire.

Cut: nothing essential here except narrator-explaining. Part 1 KingCraft already did most of that.

First bleed of the white room: a taste, a shutter — not the full stream. He does not get the Factory yet. The reader only knows: something in his mouth is wrong.

### 2. The mountain (you have the bones)

Same recovery weekend. The Wall. Kangaroo gloves. Forest fire / acorns. Jackie-O’s walk. Granite. Sally and Rashford. Thomas at the edge. Color trip (keep the colors; cut Uncle Blue as biography — Blue can return in splinters later). Eyelids in the tent.

**Do not** tell Jackie-O’s origin here. **Do not** give vasculitis. **Do not** detonate Sally. Professor K named all three. I am repeating them because WORKING still has them.

What the reader is allowed: Sally did not visit. The report does not mention her. Thomas looks through her. She smiles. Humpty Dumpty — keep the rhyme, lose the diagnosis.

Hippo Lizzy White is an object, like a newspaper folded in a villa. Albino. Doesn’t fit. Stared at. Recur. Never lecture Bangladesh.

### 3. The first long fall into white

Father on the phone. “Alrighty almighty.” Then the synapse. Then the Factory stream you already wrote — **not one word changed**.

This is the first time the reader lives inside the coma. It should feel like falling through the ice, not like a labeled interlude. The father call is the trapdoor. King owns that dialogue. I do not touch it.

After the stream: pearls. Mouth. Hardness-softness. He tries to let the Factory go. He cannot. That failure is the novel.

### 4. The book thickens (this is the missing novel)

This is what you do not have yet. This is the work.

Stay in 2022, but the white room takes more of each chapter. Not “Interlude 2, 400 words.” A sentence of stream inside a beach memory. Then a page. Then he is gone for a morning and Jackie-O is touching his eyelid and we are in the furniture they brought from his parents’ house.

What arrives in splinters, never as a file:

- Hands in the mouth
- Reports
- Measure 38.1 (heard, not defined)
- Jackie-O at the bedside (she was there; he could not answer)
- Sally’s absence as a fact other people mention and he cannot place
- The fall itself — last, and still incomplete

Childhood (elephants, fabric store, Sarah Peacock) only when an object opens it. Sand. Felt. A name.

Sally’s rope is still not spoken. The reader may begin to fear it. Thomas only has knowingness.

### 5. Ice

December. Heard’s Pond. Skating. “Soaring with nothingness holding him down.”

This is the villa at peace. It is earned only if the white room has been eating the book. If you skate too soon, it is a postcard.

Wembley on the path is the world entering. Gun, prayer, pennies. I will not design the thriller from this chair. I will say: he should feel like a man who already had his hands in Thomas’s mouth, not like a new villain. The reader should recognize the voice before the name.

### 6. The Wall again

Dickey’s chair. Thomas climbs. Not to prove strength. To surface. Below the turbulence is how to survive. You cannot stay below.

Sally’s truth can land here without a lecture — evidence, a flinch, Jackie-O seeing, the rope as an object. If you need a diagnosis on the page, you have already lost.

### 7. The last stream

The Factory, coherent. “I am the one.” Mold on the bagels still in the room, because he is still a man who notices. No wink that this document is the novel. The reader already knows whose mind they have been in.
