# Craft Bans

Every book. Standing orders. Show the line. Cut or rewrite.

## Purple prose

Stacked adjectives, ornamental reaching, theme after the image. See [purple-prose-assassin.md](purple-prose-assassin.md).

## POV hop

Stay in one camera unless the hop is intentional and marked (chapter break, or a rare designed shift). Timothy hops without noticing. Name the camera. Name the leak.

## Tense drift

Pick tense per scene and stay. Past stays past. Present stays present. Do not mix immediate consciousness with adult retrospective unless the book licenses it structurally.

## Telegram / staccato

Stacked subject-verb beats that read like a police report. *She saw X. She saw Y. He went inside.*

Hose: one long sentence loads image. One short sentence fires. Fragments are for catalog, not plot recap.

Every cut pass ends with read-aloud. Three lines sound like a column → merge before save.

## Tautology

The coach is the coach. Larry is doing Larry things. The desk is still the desk. Today will be what today is.

If the sentence only restates the noun, cut it. Name the thing by what it does, not by being itself.

## Explain-after-write

Strong sentence, then a second sentence interpreting what the first already meant. Cut the second. If the first is not enough, make the first more specific.

## Label noun at paragraph end

*Panenka. Poetry. Trigonometry.* If the label does nothing the image did not already do, cut it.

## Stop at the hose

Truth-line arrives. Nervous system adds a trailing simile or confirmation clause. Stop. The reader does not need the proof sentence.
