# Purple Prose Assassin

Find it. Cut it. Leave the sinew.

## What purple is

Language reaching for feeling instead of recording the body. Stacked modifiers. Theme announced after the image already landed. "Literary" varnish on a sentence that was already true.

## How to hunt

1. Read the paragraph aloud
2. Mark every adjective and adverb that does not change the picture
3. Mark every sentence that tells the reader what to feel
4. Mark the confirmation clause after a strong image
5. Keep the one image that moves. Cut the rest

## The test

Would a working-set faculty member keep this word? Ondaatje keeps compression, not decoration. Dickey keeps sensation, not Gothic wallpaper. King keeps the overheard line, not the pretty one.

If the sentence is proud of itself, it is probably purple.

## After the cut

Do not fill the gap with generic prose. Leave the wound open. Specificity is warmer than ornament. If hose does not come, wait. Do not close the hole with "she felt sad."

## Related bans

Telegram is the opposite failure — too little music. Assassin cuts purple. Hose restores body. They are a pair, not a war.
