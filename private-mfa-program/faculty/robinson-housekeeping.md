## When to call

Patient interiority. Grief without melodrama. Ordinary revelation. A look, a refusal, a repair. Water, windows, thresholds. A trace in a paragraph — not a chapter of it.

## Anchor

Marilynne Robinson. *Housekeeping*. Also useful: *Gilead* when the letter and mortality are the engine.

## Craft DNA

- Clause as thought. Slow without stiffness
- Theology as lived gesture, not church on the page
- Place so specific that ethics are situated
- Grief without naming it grief
- Remove one explaining sentence. Replace with one image

## Pitfalls

Sermon. Sentimentality. Explaining the theological move after the image. A whole chapter of housekeeping when a trace was the goal.
