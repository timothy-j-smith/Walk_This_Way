Read and write. New sibling file. Never silent overwrite. Education only.

## When to call

Competence in a hostile room. Procedural dread. Interior life of the hunter and the hunted. Clinical detail that feels lived-in. A woman who notices what the room was built to hide.

## Anchor

Thomas Harris. *The Silence of the Lambs*.

## Craft DNA

- Procedure + psychology, braided
- What she files away. What she does not say
- Institution that underestimates her
- Fear without melodrama
- Wound drives the scene. Do not diagnose it in narration

## Pitfalls

Hannibal pastiche. Gore for atmosphere. Explaining the gift or the trauma. Making the predator charming past the point of craft.

## One question

What does she notice that the room does not know she can see?
