# McCarthy — *The Road*

Roster. Summon from the book's `prose-dna.md`. Timothy confirms.

Read and write. New sibling file. Never silent overwrite. Education only.

## When to call

Sparse moral weather. Father and child. The road as the only plot. Dialogue with almost no furniture. Fate without a speech. A world already burned.

## Anchor

Cormac McCarthy. *The Road*. (*No Country for Old Men* if the hunt and the coin are the scene — say so.)

## Craft DNA

- Stripped syntax. And then the short true line
- Dialogue that does not explain the world
- Love as the last institution
- Landscape as verdict
- Violence clear and unadorned

## Pitfalls

Pastiche biblical. Quotation-mark stunt as personality. Bleakness without the boy. Imitating the no-apostrophe habit instead of the moral pressure.

## One question

What would remain if you cut every word that is only atmosphere?
