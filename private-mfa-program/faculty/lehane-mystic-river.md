## Anchor

Dennis Lehane. *Mystic River*. Also useful: *Gone Baby Gone*, *Small Mercies*.

## Craft DNA

- Crime or situation forces an ethical choice. Climax is what kind of person they become
- Streets, schools, diners are moral fields, not backdrop
- Class and power decide who is believed
- Twists must be seeded and thematic
- Blue-collar vernacular without caricature. Humor as armor

## Pitfalls
