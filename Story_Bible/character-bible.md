# CHARACTER BIBLE - Walk This Way

## Main Characters

### Maxanne Sartori
**Age (2024):** 73 years old  
**Role:** WBCN radio DJ, narrator of the story  
**Background:** Been with WBCN since early 1970s, discovered Aerosmith in 1973  
**Voice/Personality:** Nostalgic, wise, slightly bitter, authentic Boston voice  
**Key Traits:** 
- Radio personality who values authenticity over commercialism
- Witnessed the transformation of Boston music scene
- Understanding of class divisions and their impact on music
**Arc:** Telling the story of Nick and Jackie on WBCN's final broadcast

---

### Nick Johnson
**Age (1979):** 17 years old  
**Age (1975, middle school):** 13 years old 
**Favorite Band:** Aerosmith !!  
**Background:** Working-class kid from Dutton Road (wrong side of Hop Brook)  
**Family:** 
- Father: Dan Johnson (died in construction accident, November 14, 1977)
- Mother: Grace Johnson (works at Friendly's)
**Voice/Personality:** Working-class, passionate about music, raw talent  
**Musical Style:** Raw, emotional, Aerosmith-influenced rock  
**Key Traits:**
- Plays pawn shop guitar held together with electrical tape
- Natural rock voice, known as that "guitar guy" around town
- Lives in house where mortgage is always late
- Becomes "man of the house" at 15 after father's death
**Arc:** From innocent friendship to bitter division, brief redemption at recording session

---

### Jackie Marsh
**Age (1979):** 17 years old  
**Age (1975, middle school):** 13 years old  
**Favorite Band:** Queen !!  
**Background:** Upper-class kid from wealthy side of Hop Brook  
**Family:**
- Father: Dr. Richard Marsh (surgeon, strict, controlling)
- Mother: Elizabeth Marsh (socialite, concerned with appearances)
**Voice/Personality:** Classically trained, conflicted about advantages, wants authentic connection  
**Musical Style:** Technical precision, Queen-influenced, classical training  
**Key Traits:**
- Plays expensive guitar with perfect technique
- Educated, articulate, careful speech
- Trapped by class expectations
- Wants authentic connection but blocked by social barriers
**Arc:** From confidence to isolation, brief redemption at recording session

---

### Patty Lally
**Age (1979):** 17 years old  
**Background:** Concord kid who refuses to choose sides  
**Role:** The "frequency" keeper who remembers what others forget  
**Voice/Personality:** Authentic, grounded, emotionally intelligent  
**Key Traits:**
- Remembers the harmony that existed before division
- Acts as bridge between Nick and Jackie
- Wins Battle of the Bands with authentic performance
**Arc:** Synthesis of Nick's raw passion and Jackie's technical skill

## Character Relationships

### Nick & Jackie
- **Middle School (1974-1976):** Inseparable friends, musical partnership
- **After Dan's Death (April 1976):** Friendship fractures, class divisions emerge
- **High School (1976-1979):** Don't acknowledge each other in halls
- **Recording Session (March 1979):** Brief redemption, prove harmony still exists
- **Final Separation:** Dr. Marsh intervenes, different paths (Army vs. Juilliard)

### Maxanne & The Boys
- **1979:** Witnesses Battle of the Bands, arranges recording session
- **1980:** Receives call from Grace about Nick joining Army
- **2009:** Plays their demo tape on WBCN's final broadcast

## Character Voice DNA

### Nick's Voice
- Working-class Boston dialect
- Economical speech, defensive pride
- Raw emotion, direct expression
- Hides vulnerability behind toughness

### Jackie's Voice
- Educated, precise language
- Rarely uses contractions when nervous
- Careful, academic speech
- Emotional uncertainty hidden by precision

### Maxanne's Voice
- Radio-broadcast rhythm
- Conversational, reflective
- Nostalgic but not sentimental
- Wise observer of human nature
