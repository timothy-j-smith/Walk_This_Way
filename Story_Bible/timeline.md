# TIMELINE - Walk This Way

## 1970s - WBCN & Boston Music Scene

### 1971
- Maxanne begins wearing Sony MDR-7506 headphones (still uses them in 2009)

### 1972
- Maxanne helps promote Aerosmith at WBCN
- Steven Tyler and Aerosmith perform at WBCN Studio C

### 1974-1975 - Middle School Era
**Nick and Jackie are 13 years old**

**Early 1974:**
- Nick and Jackie meet in Curtis Middle School Cafeteria
- Nick is wearing Aerosmith belt buckle
- Jackie is wearing Queen belt buckle
- A friendship bonded by Rock Music is formed
- Nick and Jackie hate Disco Music

**May 15, 1975 - Middle School Talent Show:**
- Curtis Middle School gymnasium
- Eight acts compete
- Karen Bowers (blonde, voice lessons) performs 8th (last): "The Way You Look Tonight"
- Karen Bowers always wins and it is assumed she will win again as she has in all previous musical competitions
- Nick & Jackie perform 7th: Mashup of "Dream On" + "Somebody to Love"
- Nick & Jackie shock the audience where there rock and roll mashup
- Nick & Jackie win talent show
- Standing ovation
- Prize: One week at Camp Winnipesaukee intensive music camp program in New Hampshire
- Grace Johnson cries in audience
- Dr. Richard Marsh has mixed emotions about Jackie playing Rock and Roll, he likes to be a "winner", but Rock and Roll is an outcast to him.

### 1975

**Summer 1975:**
- Camp Winnipesaukee - [Details to be developed]
- Intensive music camp
- Nick and Jackie meet Patty Lalally from Concord Massachusetts (neighboring town to Sudbury)
- Nick and Jackie compete for Patty's attention
- Introduction of complications to Nick/Jackie friendship

**Fall 1975:**
- Freshman year in high school
- Lincoln Sudbury Regional High School introduces new groups, cliques, Rampers, Rats, Regs and Rollers
- Nick and Jackie don't see each other or hang out and play music like the did in middle school

**November 14, 1976 (Tuesday morning):**
- Sophomore year in High School
- Dan Johnson dies in construction accident
- Prudential Tower project, Back Bay Boston
- Working 37-story construction project
- Crane cable snaps at 10:17 AM
- Four steel I-beams fall onto construction trailer
- Dan was checking blueprints, eating tuna sandwich Grace made
- Had 9/16ths socket in his pocket
- OSHA investigation: cable scheduled for replacement 2 weeks earlier, delayed for cost savings
- Construction company pays $50,000 (minimum required by law)
- Insurance company fights claim for 8 months

**After Dan's Death:**
- Grace returns to Friendly's after 2 weeks bereavement leave
- Dan's tools remain on garage pegboard
- Garage becomes Nick's practice space with his band
- Rock and Roll becomes Nick's obsession & his way of dealing with grief
- Jackie reaches out, but Nick withdraws
- Jackie and Nick's friendship finally fractures for good

### 1979

**Early 1979:**
- Disco dying
- Disco Demolition Night July 12th, 1979 at Comiskey Park in Chicago
- The Clash about to "teach America that punk had something to say"
- MTV still 3 years away

**March 1979 (Weeks before Battle of Bands):**
- Maxanne hears rumors about Nick Johnson the "guitar guy" from Sudbury & his garage sessions
- Drives powder-blue Karmann Ghia to Dutton Road
- Visits Johnson house, meets Grace
- Enters garage, hears Nick's band (Nick + 3 Lincoln boys)
- Offers to record demo at WBCN after midnight
- Same week: Dr. Richard Marsh calls Maxanne about Jackie's recital as word got out about her visiting Nick Johnson
- Maxanne intrigued about hearing that both boys are from the same small town outside of Boston so she accepts

**March 1979 (Jackie's Recital):**
- Marsh house, Concord Road
- Living room transformed to concert space
- Steinway grand piano
- Audience: prep school parents, university faculty, trust fund guardians, Doctors, upper class
- Jackie's Program:
  - Chopin's Nocturne in E-flat major
  - Bach's Goldberg Variations (excerpts)
  - Debussy's "Clair de Lune"
  - Rachmaninoff's Prelude in C-sharp minor
  - [Supposed to end with] Mozart's Sonata in A major, K. 331
- Jackie stops before Mozart, plays "Under Pressure" (Queen/Bowie) instead
- Maxanne gives Jackie her card and the same offer that she presented to Nick

**March 15, 1979 - Battle of the Bands:**
- Lincoln-Sudbury Regional High School auditorium
- WBCN sponsored event
- Maxanne attends as one of judges
- Nick and Jackie compete against each other
- There is a total of 8 regional bands
- Jackie leads Queen cover band
- Nick leads Aerosmith cover band
- Patty Lally leads a Fleetwood Mac cover Band
- [More Details to be shared / developed]
- "The night I watched the future of rock and roll die in a high school auditorium"
- Patty wins the competition and convinces Nick & Jackie to reconcile if only for one night of music magic & making of final demo

## 2009 - WBCN's Final Broadcast

### August 2009 (Present-day frame)

**Final Night:**
- WBCN 104.1 FM dies at midnight
- After 56 years as "the Rock of Boston"
- Flipping to automated Christian contemporary format
- Maxanne Sartori (age 59) hosts final 4-hour broadcast
- Studio C (same studio where Aerosmoth played in '72)
- Program Director: 28 years old, thinks rock started with Nirvana
- Engineer: Young, on phone, doesn't understand significance of final broadcast and the end of WBCN
- Bourbon: Pappy Van Winkle, gift from Steven Tyler
- Note from Tyler: "For the first lady who ever gave a damn"

**During Broadcast:**
- Maxanne tells story of Nick Johnson and Jackie Marsh
- Plays previously unheard mashup of Patty, Jackie and Nick from the recording the night after the Battle of the Bands
- Maxanne closes out WBCN with Aersomith's "Walk This Way"
- [Additional details of what revelations come when during broadcast - to be determined]

**The Broadcast's Purpose:**
- Tell story of the "Loss of Innocence"
- Explain why WBCN is dying / "when American radio chose money over music"
- Boston glitters outside: "all glass towers and gentrification where dive bars used to breed bands"
- The Rat (club) is now luxury condos: "2.4 million for 2,400 sq ft"

## Places Timeline

### Boston Music Venues
- **WBCN Studio C:** Where Maxanne works, where demos recorded
- **The Rat (Kenmore Square):** Legendary dive club, now luxury condos
- **Symphony Hall:** Referenced as contrast to garage bands

### Sudbury Geography
- **Hop Brook:** Divides town between working-class and upper-class
- **The Dump:** instersects with Hop Brook and creates Sudbury Quadrants
- **Dutton Road:** Working-class side (Nick's house)
- **Concord Road:** Upper-class side (Jackie's house)
- **Lincoln-Sudbury Regional High School:** Serves both Lincoln and Sudbury and all kids gravitate to Rampers, Rats, Regs and Rollers
- **20 miles west of Boston**
