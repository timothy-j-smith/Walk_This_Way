# Michelle "Ice" Edwards — Basketball Career Research

**Born:** March 6, 1966, Boston, Massachusetts  
**Height:** 5'9" | **Position:** Guard  
**Nickname:** "Ice" (earned for her clutch shooting ability)

---

## High School: Cathedral High School, Boston, MA (graduated 1984)

- **First girl in Massachusetts history to score over 2,000 points** in a high school career
- Named **Catholic League Division 3 High School Player of the Year** twice
- Led Cathedral High School to **two Eastern Massachusetts Division 3 Basketball Championships** (1983, 1984)
- Inducted into the high school Hall of Fame
- Notably, basketball was not her first sport — she initially preferred **tennis and skiing** and came to basketball relatively late

---

## College: University of Iowa Hawkeyes (1984–1988)

Played under Hall of Fame coach **C. Vivian Stringer**.

### Career Stats
- 117 games played | 102-22 record
- 1,821 points (4th all-time at Iowa) — 15.6 PPG career average
- 431 assists (2nd all-time at Iowa) — 3.7 APG
- 235 steals (3rd all-time at Iowa)
- 4.1 RPG | 53.5% FG | 69.9% FT

### Senior Season (1987–88)
- 20.0 PPG, 4.8 RPG, 4.5 APG, 2.9 SPG
- Led Iowa to a **29-2 record** (17-1 Big Ten) and the program's **first-ever #1 national ranking**

### Honors & Awards
- **1988 Champion Products National Player of the Year** (highest honor in women's college basketball)
- 1988 Kodak All-American
- 1988 Naismith All-American
- 1988 USBWA All-American
- 1987–88 Big Ten Player of the Year
- 3x First-Team All-Big Ten
- 2x NCAA All-Region selection
- University of Iowa Athlete of the Year (1988)
- Iowa Athlete of the Decade
- **Only female Hawkeye to have her jersey retired (#30)** — hung in Carver-Hawkeye Arena, March 1990
- Named one of Iowa's **"Top 50 Athletes of the Century"**
- Inducted into the University of Iowa Athletics Hall of Fame (2000)

### Recruiting Story
Edwards had no idea where Iowa was. Coach Stringer visited her in Boston and asked her to pull out a map. Once Edwards visited Iowa City, the natural friendliness of the people and the academic reputation won her over. She later said: "I would definitely go back to Iowa today. No regrets at all."

---

## International / USA Basketball

- Member of the **United States Select Team** (1986)
- **1991 Pan American Games** (Havana, Cuba) — won **bronze medal** with the U.S. women's national basketball team

---

## Professional: Italy (1988–1997)

After graduating from Iowa, Edwards played **nine seasons professionally in Italy**, competing for clubs including **Faenza, Pistoia, Ferrara, and Pavia**.

- Won **three Italian League All-Star Game MVP awards**
- Named **1997 Italian League All-Star Game MVP**
- Later reflected on the loneliness of the extended eight-month stints overseas
- Had considered retiring before the WNBA was established

---

## WNBA Career (1997–2001)

### Cleveland Rockers (1997–2000)
- Selected in the **1997 WNBA Inaugural Allocation Draft** — one of only **16 players assigned as franchise players** to each team
- Selected **2nd overall** in the allocation
- 1997 WNBA Sportsmanship Award runner-up
- Named to the **1997 WNBA All-Star European Travel Team**

## Novel-Relevant Notes

- **Boston roots** — grew up in Boston, played at Cathedral High School
- **Late bloomer in basketball** — started with tennis and skiing
- **"Ice" nickname** — clutch performer, cool under pressure
- **Pioneer** — first Massachusetts girl to break 2,000 HS points; one of 16 original WNBA franchise players
- **Italy years (1988–1997)** — nine lonely seasons abroad, nearly retired before WNBA saved her career
- **Stringer connection** — played for C. Vivian Stringer at Iowa, later worked for her at Rutgers
- **Jersey #30 retired** — only female Hawkeye so honored
