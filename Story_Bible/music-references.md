# MUSIC REFERENCES - Walk This Way

## Songs Used in Story

### Aerosmith - "Dream On"
**From:** First album (1973)  
**Used:** 
- Nick plays in garage (Ch 2)
- Nick & Jackie practice together (1975)
- Part of middle school talent show mashup (Ch 6)
- Maxanne cues it up during broadcast

**Significance:** Working-class anthem, Steven Tyler's signature, represents Nick's musical identity

**Lyrics Referenced:**
- "Every time that I look in the mirror, all these lines on my face getting clearer"
- "Half my life's in black and white photographs"
- "Dream on, dream on..."

### Queen - "Somebody to Love"
**Used:**
- Jackie wants to play this (vs. Nick wanting "Dream On")
- Part of middle school talent show mashup (Ch 6)

**Significance:** 
- Gospel-meets-opera-meets-rock
- Freddie Mercury's theatrical style

- Complex harmonies appeal to Jackie's technical skill

- "Can anybody find me somebody to love?"

## The 93-Second Demo

**Recording:** Nick & Jackie, age 13 (1975)  
**Quality:** Scratchy, tape hiss, left channel crackles above 6  
**Content:** Two young voices, acoustic guitars, mashup arrangement  
**Significance:** 
- Only surviving recording of them playing together
- "All the evidence I have that America chose division over music"
- Cuts off mid-verse
- Maxanne plays it on final broadcast

## Music as Theme

### Key Thematic Uses of Music:

**Democracy's Wild Card:**
- "Music has always been democracy's wild card"
- Crosses class boundaries that America builds
- "The frequency that ignores the boundaries we build"

**Survival vs. Decoration:**
- Nick: "Music isn't decoration—it's survival"
- Jackie: Music as escape from expectations
- Both: Music as way to be heard/seen as themselves

**Authenticity vs. Training:**
- Nick: Raw, emotional, garage-taught
- Jackie: Technically perfect, expensively trained
- Together: Proof that both can enhance each other

**The Pivot Point (1979):**
- "When rock and roll had to decide what it wanted to be"
- Corporations circling
- MTV 3 years away
- "Last moments when two kids with talent and rage could matter"

## Song Selection Guidelines

**For Maxanne's Broadcast:**
- Songs should be iconic 1970s rock
- Boston connection preferred
- Each song should underscore the emotional moment
- Vinyl/analog quality emphasized

**For Character Identity:**
- Nick = raw rock (Aerosmith, working-class anthems)
- Jackie = theatrical/complex (Queen, classical influences)
- Together = harmony possible across differences
