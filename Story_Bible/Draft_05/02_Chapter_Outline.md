## Act I — *Toys in the Attic* (The Frequency)

### Chapter 1 — "Wish You Were Here" ✅

**Job:** Seat the reader in the **2009 booth** — four-hour clock, last broadcast, cassette as loaded gun. Establish Maxanne's voice through **objects** (headphones, bourbon, Tyler note), not thesis. Promise the story; **do not** pre-spoil the boys' arc with interpretive sentences. Overture only.

### Chapter 2 — "Draw the Line" ✅

**Job:** First **brick** — Maxanne to the garage; music felt through boots before heard. Plant **toolbox empty slot** and Johnson grief without opening it. Boys holding instruments "like drowning people hold wreckage." End on image, not moral.

### Chapter 3 — "Killer Queen" ✅

**Job:** Jackie's **gilded cage** — recital room, father's curling pinky (hand motif born). Queen detonation as identity, not rebellion speech. Class through comedy ("pearls clutching themselves"). Father's hand says everything; narrator must not.

### Chapter 4 — "More Than a Feeling" ✅

**Job:** **Town as character** — Hop Brook, four quarters, Dump, pigs, stone walls. Geography *is* class; zero lecture. Load-bearing wall for whole novel. End on human (Patty seed) where possible; accept length if every sentence earns.

### Chapter 5 — "School's Out" ✅

**Job:** **Origin of friendship** — Sept 1974 cafeteria, belt buckles, argument-as-courtship. Pure dialogue brick. Two boys discover they need each other to argue. Patty absent (by design). Stop before "He could. He would." if scalpel pass allows.

### Chapter 6 — "With a Little Help From My Friends" ✅

**Job:** Deepen friendship — cassettes, conservation land, **first locked jam** (engine/wingspan: use once). Grace meets Jackie's manners; Dan hears through wall. Doug Malone = friendship has cost. Crown-molding seed (Grace's father's house, not Dan's).

### Chapter 7 — "Bohemian Rhapsody" ✅

**Job:** **Marsh house excavation** — 68°, ruler breakfast, attic sheet music, Elizabeth. "Practice Queen like you're hiding a body." Hose line **once**: classical vs rock breath. Father's hand on cloth. No second thesis ring later in book.

### Chapter 8 — "Mama Kin" ✅

**Job:** **Johnson house mirror** — Dan's guitar through floor; crown molding under landlord white. Grace's buried gentility rhymes Marsh sealed piano **without connecting aloud**. Music through wall = prayer. **Withhold toolbox contents** for Ch 15.

### Chapter 9 — "Just What I Needed" ✅

**Job:** Frame — Maxanne decides **1975 talent show** entry; "nobody knows it except us." **Do not** narrate the show's outcome (that's Ch 10). Compress to decision + hinge; protect brick suspense.

### Chapter 10 — "Dream On" ✅

**Job:** **Talent show set piece** — mashup bar-by-bar, three-second silence, Dr. Marsh's hand opens once. Show parents' bodies (woman with program), not "everyone knew." Peak Act I spectacle; friendship still alive on stage.

### Chapter 11 — "Radio, Radio" ✅

**Job:** Maxanne's **own** origin — Brickyard, Aerosmith, Springsteen "HI MOM." Frame authorized because she lived it. Plant frequency motif. Compress self-indictment (1973 share vs 1979 use) to sharpest two sentences.

### Chapter 12 — "Summer Breeze" ✅

**Job:** **Camp Elbanobscot** — Sudbury sleepaway, sacred barn vs wilderness, Patty **first meeting**, shared 14th birthday, a cappella triad click. Bunk confession (toolbox / attic) without sap on instruments. Patty smell: plant once, hard.

### Chapter 13 — "Breakdown" ✅

**Job:** **Friendship fracture brick** — freshman fall 1975, forty feet linoleum, parallel doors, scratch-on-record image. Canyon metaphor earned; cut gloss on leaves. Most important architecture in Act I; rhythm guardrail born here.

### Chapter 14 — "Baba O'Riley" ✅

**Job:** **School social map** — April 1976 mud, shop/Carl, Glass Corridor, Rampers, Patty flute pass-by. Teenage wasteland; fracture **deepening**, not explained. Grace = **thread only** (~10 lines max); full Friendly's → Ch 23.

### Chapter 15 — "Landslide"

**Job:** **Dan's death** (Nov 1977) + **toolbox opens** — ordinary morning, Globe obituary class detail, inheritance physical. Grace: "He gave it to you instead." **End at closed lid** — cut explanatory paragraphs after. Act I curtain; Nick man-of-house.
