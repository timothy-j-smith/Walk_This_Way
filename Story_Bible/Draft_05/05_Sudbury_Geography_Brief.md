**Purpose:** Worldbuilding reference for maps, illustrations, Claude/ Hyper Agent prompts, etc. **Fiction only** — not survey-accurate.

**Author geography lock (6/2026):** Four quarters = **literary lens**, not a zoning plat. Hop Brook (hard divide) · middle conservation strip (west trails / east dump+pig) · Route 20 + Route 117 parallel. Synced with `Draft_05/PARKING_LOT.md`.

## Draw these first (layer order)

### 1. Hop Brook — the hard divide

- Runs **north–south** through the center of town
- Divides **west** (NW + SW) from **east** (NE + SE)
- Real water: brown in April, whisper in July; plank bridge; granite ledges
- Primary **class** metaphor — the only line that is truly geographic

### 2. Middle strip — colonial conservation (horizontal belt)

- Has been **protected land since colonial settlement** (~250+ years) — older than most roads
- Runs **east–west** through the center of town, **perpendicular to Hop Brook**
- **West half of strip (NW | SW):** pristine conservation — pine, scrub oak, colonial **fieldstone walls** (no mortar), deer paths, mini-bike ruts, Camp Elbanobscot on the edge (Route 20 + reservoir)
- **East half of strip (NE | SE):** same land on the map, different fate — **town dump** and **Cavicchio pig farm side by side** (smell, trench, reclaim corner). Working-class side got what the wealthy side wouldn't accept next to their trails
- Separates **north from south** within each half of town:
  - West: conservation belt between **NW and SW**
  - East: dump/farm margin between **NE and SE**

### 3. Route 20 — Boston Post Road (northern connector)

- Runs **parallel** to the middle strip, **above** it (northern band)
- Connects **NW ↔ NE** — the northern pair of quarters
- Main commercial spine; extend east = **Boston hub**
- **Friendly's** sits on Route 20 near Hop Brook — town commons on paper; Grace knows the name is a lie when you're on the clock (polyester, GRACE tag, fryer oil)
- Camp Elbanobscot: conservation between Route 20 and reservoir (Ch 12)

### 3b. Route 217 — local strip (author lock)

- **Store 24** (Gordon family — backdrop seed only) sits on Route 217, not Route 20
- Nick buys strings there; Ch 2 register beat when planted (**not** Grace milk run — cut Ch 23 quarry coda 6/14)

### 4. Route 117 — southern connector

- Runs **parallel to Route 20**, **below** the middle strip (southern band)
- Connects **SW ↔ SE** — the southern pair of quarters
- Extend east = **Boston hub** (same logic as 20 — two parallel arteries)
- **L-S Regional High** ties to Route 117 — brick and glass at the SE edge, between 117 and Hop Brook's last loop; **four doors**; shop wing where **Mr. Joseph (Joe) Pacenka** teaches auto shop (Nick's school father figure — he lives in the building, not a NE neighborhood)

## Four quarters (conceptual, clockwise from NW)

### NW — Old money

- Revolutionary-era wealth; colonials; brass knockers; dogs trained quiet
- Hand-laid stone walls; expensive quiet
- **Grace's family before the scandal** — she was a Ramper at L-S; she does **not** live here in 1979

### NE — Working hands (Nick's world)

- **Dutton Road** — Johnson house, garage, crown molding under landlord white
- Dan & Grace now; Nick; Rollers; pawn shop; worn trucks
- Route 20 / Boston Post Road — Friendly's (Grace works); community center (Ch 13)

### SW — Professional rise (Jackie's world)

- **Concord Road** — Marsh house; Steinway; breakfast at 68°
- **Dr. Richard Marsh — social climber:** lives SW (professor, cultivated) but **wants NW** (old money where Grace fell from). Ascent, not arrival. House-hunting in NW = optional future beat, not required on page
- **Jackie** — closed fallboard; volume the house can't tolerate

### SE — Margin (Patty's world)

- **Lally house** — general SE area near dump/farm smell, not "the pig kid"
- **Cavicchio pig farm** — adjacent to **town dump**; smell in wash and schoolbooks
- **Town Dump** — acres, not a ribbon; reclaim corner (Patty's blue Schwinn born here); Saturday wagons from all quarters; *"Nobody's garbage was better than anyone else's"*
- **L-S High** — everyone attends; Patty belongs to no clique
- **Memorial Congregational Church** — Sudbury Center; Patty/Rumours rehearsal space (Ch 28+). Rev. Goff, key under mat. **Not St. Michael's** (Concord carryover — cut all references)

## Patty — home, school, and the bike

**Home:** SE general area — dump and pig farm **next to each other** in the east half of the middle strip; smell is the price of the air.

**Dad's work:** Fixes furnaces, mowers, outboards on **every side of Hop Brook** — Patty has stood in all four kitchens. Geography is **knowledge**, not just address.

**School commute (canon-friendly):**

- **Short way:** SE to L-S at the SE edge — stays inside her quarter. Many kids take this. Fine for background.
- **Patty's way (character):** She **does not always take the short way.** Ch 4 is a **wander**, not a commute — brook trail, landfill road, stone walls, perimeter paths. She rides the **long edge** — along conservation, past reclaim, along Route 117 toward the school — collecting the town the way you know a face you've looked at all your life.
- **Map in her head vs. map on asphalt:** The four quarters live in how she **reads** Sudbury (trails on no town-hall plat). She *can* cross all four on a bike; she often **chooses** the loop that grazes the other quarters' edges without cutting through their centers — apart by temperament, not rejection.

**Do not** imply she never leaves SE — imply she **knows** the whole town and **routes** through it on her terms.

**Rehearsals:** **Memorial Congregational Church**, Sudbury Center — white clapboard, honor-roll stained glass, Rev. Goff lets Rumours use the sanctuary Tuesday/Thursday evenings (key under mat). **Author lock 6/14:** **Not St. Michael's** (Concord/neighbor-town carryover). Alternate if author prefers Catholic continuity: **St. Anselm Parish** — ask before switching.
