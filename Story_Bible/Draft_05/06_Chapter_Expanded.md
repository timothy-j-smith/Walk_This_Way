## Act I — *Toys in the Attic*

**Chapter 1 — "Wish You Were Here"**  
Maxanne Sartori opens WBCN's final four-hour broadcast in Studio B (August 2009): bourbon, taped Sony headphones, the ON AIR sign, and the unauthorized cassette she has carried since 1979. She promises the story of two Sudbury boys who should have changed everything and didn't — without yet playing the tape. **Pass 2 (Professor O, 7/11/2026):** P1 canon fixed; scalpel/hose — cut thesis gloss, bourbon toast, Kid triple-look, orphan quote; trust cassette + pegboard.

**Chapter 2 — "Draw the Line"**  
Maxanne drives to Dutton Road and hears Nick's guitar through the garage before she sees him; Jackie is there too, instruments held like wreckage. The red toolbox sits unopened; Dan's empty socket slot on the Maxwell House lid. First brick of the boys' sound and the Johnson grief the town doesn't name.

**Chapter 3 — "Killer Queen"**  
Jackie's recital-room world: father's hand on the piano, pearls and 68° discipline, Queen as precision identity. Dr. Marsh calls WBCN about the upcoming recital. The octave-reaching pinky plants the Marsh hand motif without explanation.

**Chapter 4 — "More Than a Feeling"**  
Patty Lally rides Sudbury's hidden geography — Hop Brook, four quarters, conservation trails, dump-and-pig east half — and reads the town as class without lecture. First Patty POV; the blue reclaimed bike and the Schwinn at L-S. Load-bearing map chapter for the whole novel.

**Chapter 5 — "School's Out"**  
September 1974 cafeteria: Nick's Aerosmith buckle meets Jackie's Queen buckle; argument becomes courtship. Disco contempt as shared language. Two boys discover they need each other to disagree.

**Chapter 6 — "With a Little Help From My Friends"**  
Friendship deepens through TDK bootlegs, conservation-land walks, and the first locked jam in Dan's garage (engine/wingspan image — use once). Grace meets Jackie's manners; Dan hears through the wall at night. Doug Malone warns friendship has a cost.

**Chapter 7 — "Bohemian Rhapsody"**  
Inside the Marsh house: ruler breakfast, attic sheet music, Elizabeth's ease, "practice Queen like you're hiding a body." Father's hand on cloth. One hose line on classical breath vs rock breath — do not repeat elsewhere.

**Chapter 8 — "Mama Kin"**  
Mirror chapter at the Johnson house: Dan coaches Nick's *Walk This Way* riff by day; original songs through the floor at night. Crown molding under landlord white; Grace's buried gentility rhymes the sealed piano without connecting aloud. Toolbox withheld for Ch 15.

**Chapter 9 — "Just What I Needed"**  
Frame: Maxanne rewinds to the **decision** to enter the 1975 talent show — "nobody knows it except us." Does **not** narrate the outcome (Ch 10 owns the gymnasium). Folding chairs tease at chapter end.

**Chapter 10 — "Dream On"**  
Ephraim Curtis talent show set piece: *Walk This Way* into *Bohemian Rhapsody* mashup bar-by-bar; three-second silence; Dr. Marsh's hand opens once in his lap. Dan on his feet in work boots. Friendship still alive on stage at peak Act I spectacle.

**Chapter 11 — "Radio, Radio"**  
Maxanne's authorized origin — Brickyard, Panasonic recorder, Aerosmith discovery, Springsteen "HI MOM." Frequency motif and the difference between sharing conviction and selling a room.

**Chapter 12 — "Summer Breeze"**  
Camp Elbanobscot: sacred barn vs wilderness, Patty's first meeting with both boys, shared fourteenth birthday, a cappella triad click. Bunk confessions (toolbox / attic) without instrument sap. Patty smell planted once.

**Chapter 13 — "Breakdown"**  
Freshman fall 1975 fracture brick: forty feet of linoleum, parallel doors, scratch-on-record image. The canyon metaphor earned. Most important architecture in Act I; rhythm guardrail born here.

**Chapter 14 — "Baba O'Riley"**  
April 1976 school social map — mud, shop/Carl, Glass Corridor rats, Rampers platform; Patty guitar pass-by. Grace thread only (~10 lines); full Friendly's deferred to Ch 23. Teenage wasteland; fracture deepening without explanation.

**Chapter 15 — "Landslide"**  
November 1977: Dan's ordinary morning, crane cable, Globe paragraph with misspelled name, funeral, toolbox opens. Grace: "He gave it to you instead." Act I curtain; Nick becomes man of the house. End at closed lid.
