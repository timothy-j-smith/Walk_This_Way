MFA Workspace Template
Timothy J. Smith's private MFA writing workspace. For education and learning. Not for sharing. Not for publication-as-MFA-product.

What this is
A clean, copy-pastable Cursor workspace template for one novel at a time.
The template is the empty room — methodology, six Author MCP professors, ABSH stages as commands, rules that respect how you work. You copy this folder for every new novel and the new copy becomes that novel's home.
The previous Cursor mess does not live here. This is the clean slate.

How to spin up a new novel from the template
# Replace <NovelName> with the actual title (use kebab-case, no spaces)
cp -R ~/MFA_Template ~/Novels/<NovelName>
cd ~/Novels/<NovelName>
git init
git add .
git commit -m "Spin up <NovelName> from MFA_Template"
Then open ~/Novels/<NovelName>/ as a Cursor workspace.
Inside the new workspace, open DASHBOARD.md and fill in:
Novel name
Primary professor (King, Ondaatje, or Dickey)
Current ABSH stage
Word-count target
That is the entire spin-up. No re-explaining. No re-configuring.


The six professors
You have three core professors, any of whom can be the primary for a given novel:
Professor K — Stephen King. Anchor: Blind Willie / Hearts in Atlantis (non-horror King). Plainspoken kid-narrator interior. Working-class New England realism.
Professor O — Michael Ondaatje. Anchor: The English Patient. Lyric compression, fragmented memory, identity under pressure, nested time and place.
Professor D — James Dickey. Anchor: Deliverance. Muscular kinetic prose, deep-third interiority, nature as moral pressure, aftermath as craft.
You have three guests, who are invited for paragraph-level or sentence-level moments — never as primary:
Guest R — Marilynne Robinson. Quiet sentences, time-as-theology, slow attention.
Guest L — Dennis Lehane. Hardboiled-yet-tender, place as character, neighborhood truth.
Guest Ke — Ken Kesey. Voice-driven, mythic-scale, chorus and madness.
Rule: one primary per novel. Guests join only when the craft calls for them. No professor-hopping mid-chapter unless the scene demands it and you ask.

ABSH — your method
A — Architecture. Outline, characters, timeline, theme. The structural skeleton.
B — Brick. The rough draft. The story comes through. You are the mechanism.
S — Scalpel. Cut. Remove what does not earn its place.
H — Hose. Add only what is missing. Not decoration. Not safety prose.
Scalpel and Hose loop iteratively. Architecture and Brick are mostly one-pass; Scalpel-Hose is the conscious craft.
See ABSH_METHODOLOGY.md for the long version.


Folder layout
<NovelName>/
├── README.md                       (this file, customize per novel)
├── DASHBOARD.md                    (live status of the novel)
├── ABSH_METHODOLOGY.md             (the four stages, the loop, the mechanism)
├── .cursor/
│   ├── agents/                     (six professors + mfa-architect)
│   ├── rules/                      (ABSH, neurodiversity, honesty, voice-intactness)
│   ├── commands/                   (/architecture /brick /scalpel /hose /summon-professor /voice-check)
│   └── config/                     (mcp.json — lean, writing-only)
├── 01_architecture/                (outline, characters, timeline, theme)
├── 02_brick/                       (rough draft chapters and notes)
├── 03_scalpel/                     (revision passes)
├── 04_hose/                        (additions only)
├── research/                       (corpus excerpts and craft notes)
└── deliverables/                   (final manuscripts and exports)

Save path
Local: ~/Novels/<NovelName>/
Canonical: GitHub repo per novel
Backup: NAS nightly mirror
Avoid: iCloud Drive (sync issues caused past data loss)
