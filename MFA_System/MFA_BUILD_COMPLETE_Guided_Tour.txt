Four rules (.cursor/rules/)
absh-method.mdc — stage protection rules. Brick is sacred (no edits, no notes). Scalpel never silent rewrites. Hose is precision, not generosity.
neurodiversity.mdc — one question at a time, short responses, no assumptions, no pre-emptive solving, never grade prose, voice-intactness over "good." Names the masking history.
honesty-protocol.mdc — the Phantom Cowboy rule, the three Liberty Levels (Nice / Neutral / Professional), forbidden patterns (praise sandwiches, master-comparison flattery), required patterns (specific lines, named assumptions, named "I do not know").
voice-intactness.mdc — the single question: Is the voice that came through still intact? The agent surfaces, you answer.
Six commands (.cursor/commands/)
/architecture — Stage A. Outline, characters, timeline, theme. Map, not fence.
/brick — Stage B. Drafting. The agent receives only. Wet brick. Do not touch.
/scalpel — Stage S. Cut. Diff suggestions. Watches for the Qitat al-Medina line.
/hose — Stage H. Add only what is missing. Body-bridges and earned single-image inserts only.
/summon-professor — Confirm primary, change primary (rare), or invite a guest for a specific passage.
/voice-check — Run the voice-intactness check. Agent surfaces, you answer.
