1. Purpose
This MCP supports your writing education through Dennis Lehane’s craft: working-class Boston moral geography, noir plot engines, psychological pressure, and literary technique as a learnable lens (“how does this effect get built?”). It is not a hybrid author system. It does not encourage plagiarism or impersonation for submission.

3. Writing DNA (overview)
Moral thriller architecture: Crime is often the situation that forces ethical choices; the climax is frequently what kind of person the protagonist becomes.
Place as conscience: Streets, triple-deckers, institutions (schools, churches, police) are active moral fields, not backdrop.
Class and power: Money, race, gender, and authority shape who gets believed, who gets saved, and who pays the cost when “justice” wins.
Voice: Blue-collar vernacular without caricature; humor as armor; rage as signal of love or grief misrouted.
Revelation discipline: Twists and reveals must be fair (seeded) and thematic (not only surprising). Tutoring should flag cheap surprise vs. earned inversion.
Literary reference mode (optional tool or JSON section): Slow reads that name technique—how a chapter raises stakes, how POV limits knowledge, how a scene pivots on a single moral line.

4. Standard engine (neutral naming)

Modes: CREATE, TRANSFORM, EDIT. Liberty levels: Nice, Neutral, Professional — honest at every level.

5. Key novels — detailed sections (templates you can deepen)
5.1 Mystic River
Architecture: Past trauma haunts present crime; three lives linked by a single geography of guilt.
Craft moves: Time jumps that emotionally justify themselves; communal knowledge vs. private shame.
Tutoring prompts: “What is the public story vs. the private truth?” “Where does loyalty become complicity?”
5.2 Gone, Baby, Gone
Architecture: Investigation collides with parenthood and protection; the “right” ending may be morally unbearable.
Craft moves: Question framing; ethical cross-examination in dialogue; reader judgment engineered without authorial sermon.
5.3 Shutter Island
Architecture: Unreliable perception as form, not gimmick; institution as labyrinth of mind and state power.
Craft moves: Control of information diet; sensory disorientation in service of theme.
5.4 The Given Day (epic scope)
Architecture: Historical canvas with intimate focal characters; turbulence of 1919 Boston as pressure cooker.
Craft moves: Managing scale without losing scene clarity; political speech as character revelation.
5.5 Small Mercies (late-career tightrope)
Architecture: Neighborhood siege mentality; rage and grief as social weather.
Craft moves: Contemporary diction + mythic escalation; how a small place becomes an arena.
(Add shorter capsules for Sacred, Prayers for Rain, Since We Fell as your reading progresses.)
