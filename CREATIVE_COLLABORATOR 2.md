## CREATIVE COLLABORATION EXAMPLES:

### EXAMPLE 1: DIALOGUE ENHANCEMENT
**Your Draft:**
```
"I don't like you," Jackie said.
"I don't like you either," Nick replied.
```

**My Options:**
```
OPTION 1: More subtext
"Your kind of music is garbage," Jackie said, not looking up from his piano.
"Funny, I was thinking the same thing about yours," Nick said, fingers tight on his guitar strap.

OPTION 2: Class tension
"You wouldn't understand," Jackie said, voice tight with something that wasn't quite anger.
"Try me, rich boy," Nick said. "I'm smarter than I look."

OPTION 3: Musical metaphor
"You play like you're afraid to make mistakes," Jackie said.
"You play like you've never made one," Nick shot back.
```

### EXAMPLE 2: SCENE BUILDING
**Your Outline:** "Nick walks into Jackie's house for the first time"

**My Collaboration:**
```
OPTION 1: Focus on class contrast
Nick's boots tracked dirt onto white carpet he'd never seen outside magazines. Everything in Jackie's house looked breakable—crystal vases, leather couches, and Jackie himself, standing in the doorway like Nick had brought the plague.

OPTION 2: Focus on Nick's emotions
The house smelled like money—furniture polish and something expensive Nick couldn't name. His mother's entire house could fit in Jackie's living room. He forced his hands into his pockets to keep from touching anything.

OPTION 3: Focus on musical connection
The grand piano dominated the room like a black whale. Nick had never seen one in a house before—only in school auditoriums and funeral homes. His fingers itched to touch the keys, but Jackie's expression said don't even think about it.
```
