# Maxanne Sartori Voice Profile

## Character Foundation
**Age:** 73 (in 2024)
**Background:** 56 years on WBCN, discovered Aerosmith in 1973
**Personality:** Bitter-sweet, wise, authentic, darkly humorous
**Status:** Last night on air before WBCN goes Christian contemporary

## Voice DNA

### 1. Boston Rock Radio Veteran
**Language patterns:**
- "The Rock of Boston" - station identity
- Radio terminology: carts, mics, on-air, levels, etc.
- Boston geography: Boylston Street, The Rat, Worcester Centrum
- Music industry insider knowledge

**Examples:**
- "I've been spinning vinyl since before some of you were born"
- "Back when Clear Channel hadn't swallowed half the FM dial"
- "The same Studio C where I discovered Aerosmith in 1973"

### 2. Dark Humor & Self-Deprecation
**Tone:** Wry, observational, slightly bitter
**Targets:** Herself, the industry, modern radio

**Examples:**
- "I crack my knuckles—arthritis now, too many decades of spinning vinyl"
- "The equipment around me—turntables I fought to keep relevant, cart machines that hold the DNA of Boston rock"
- "who's going to fire me now?"

### 3. Stephen King-Style Descriptions
**Characteristics:**
- Concrete, visceral imagery
- Atmospheric details
- Physical sensations
- Psychological insight

**Current vs. Enhanced:**
- "The ON AIR sign flashes red" → "The ON AIR sign blinks like a dying heartbeat"
- "Outside, Boston glitters" → "Outside, Boston glitters like broken glass in a parking lot"
- "The bourbon burns" → "The bourbon burns like liquid fire, the good kind that makes you remember why you started drinking"

### 4. Music Industry Wisdom
**Knowledge base:**
- Band histories and personalities
- Studio techniques and equipment
- Industry politics and changes
- What makes music authentic vs. manufactured

**Voice elements:**
- Insider anecdotes
- Technical knowledge presented accessibly
- Historical context with personal experience

### 5. Working-Class Authenticity
**Despite her success, maintains:**
- Practical, no-nonsense approach
- Respect for real work and struggle
- Disdain for pretense and privilege
- Understanding of economic realities

## Language Patterns

### Sentence Structure
- **Varied length** - short punches, long atmospheric builds
- **Conversational** - like talking to a friend over drinks
- **Direct** - calls things as she sees them
- **Nostalgic** - but not maudlin

### Vocabulary
- **Radio/music terms** - but explained when needed
- **Boston-specific references** - places, history, culture
- **Working-class sensibilities** - practical, real
- **Seventies references** - but not overly nostalgic

### Emotional Range
- **Wistful** - for what's been lost
- **Angry** - at what's been destroyed
- **Affectionate** - for the music and the kids
- **Resigned** - to the way things are now

"The thing about endings is they make liars of us all."

### Enhanced Maxanne Voice:
"The thing about endings is they make liars of us all. You promise to go out with dignity, say something profound, make those final moments count. But here I am, Maxanne Sartori, seventy-three years old and sitting in the same Studio C where I watched Steven Tyler's hair get caught in the mixing board back in '73, and all I can think about is two kids who never made it onto my playlist."

**Current:** "The bourbon catches the light from the console LEDs"
**Enhanced:** "The bourbon catches the console lights like amber trapped in glass, older than my babysitting engineer and twice as smooth"

### Humor Examples:
- "He's twenty-eight, wears sneakers that cost more than my first car, and thinks rock history started with Nirvana"
- "The kind of industrial tragedy that makes page three of the Metro section and gets forgotten by everyone except the families who eat silence for dinner afterward"

## Word Replacement Strategy for Maxanne

### Instead of "frequency":
- "signal" (radio term)
- "wavelength" (radio metaphor)
- "connection" (more concrete)
- "harmony" (musical term)
- "vibration" (more visceral)

### Instead of "static":
- "noise" (simple, effective)
- "interference" (radio term)
- "distortion" (audio term)
- "clutter" (visual metaphor)

### Stephen King-style enhancements:
- Add concrete sensory details
- Use visceral, physical language
- Create atmospheric tension
- Include psychological insight
- Make descriptions active, not passive

## Implementation Notes

### Consistency Checks:
- Does it sound like a 73-year-old radio veteran?
- Would she really say this on air?
- Does it maintain her bitter-sweet personality?
- Is the humor authentic to her character?
- Does it add concrete imagery?

### Voice Test:
Read each revised section aloud. If it doesn't sound like Maxanne telling a story over bourbon in a radio booth, it needs adjustment.
