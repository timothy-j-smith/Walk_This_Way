# Word Replacement Strategy for "Walk This Way"

**Current usage:** Overused as metaphor for connection/harmony
**Problem:** Too abstract, becomes cliché

**Replacement Options:**
- **Connection** - more concrete
- **Harmony** - musical term that fits
- **Vibration** - more visceral
- **Resonance** - deeper meaning
- **Wavelength** - radio metaphor that fits Maxanne
- **Channel** - radio terminology
- **Signal** - technical but accessible
- **Tone** - musical/radio term
- **Pitch** - musical term
- **Wave** - simple, effective

**Replacement Options:**

- **Noise** - simple, effective
- **Distortion** - audio term
- **Crackle** - more specific sound
- **Hiss** - specific audio description
- **White noise** - technical but understandable
- **Clutter** - visual metaphor
- **Chaos** - emotional term
- **Confusion** - clearer meaning
- **Disruption** - active term
