# WALK THIS WAY — Parking Lot

A holding pen for ideas, seeds, themes, and good lines we cut but don't want to lose. Nothing here is committed to the manuscript. We pull from it deliberately, when and if a piece earns its place.

## How to use this

- When we cut a line we actually like, it lands here under "Homeless gems."
- When an idea is good but would dilute the current chapter, it parks here as a "Seed."
- When something needs sharpening later, it parks here as an "Open thread."
- Each entry notes the chapter it relates to, so we know where to look when we get there.

## Narration contract (decided)

- Two clean voices, two clean tenses.
- In the booth (2009): first person, present tense. Maxanne's "I" — direct, immediate, only what she knows, saw, or was told.
- Out of the booth (1979): third person, past tense. An invisible narrator that may enter any room (Jackie's bedroom, Dr. Marsh's chest). No "I," and no Maxanne commentary or interpretation leaking in.
- Working rule: the moment a 1979 third-person scene starts explaining what something means, that is Maxanne's voice crossing the seam — cut it. De-glossing and POV purity are the same fix.
- Optional advanced move (parked, not required now): one quiet acknowledgment that Maxanne is partly imagining the rooms she was never in ("I am making him up, because I need to") would license the omniscience and deepen the book. Ondaatje territory. Revisit later if wanted.
