KEY DIFFERENCES SUMMARY
================================================================================

LEHANE TECHNIQUES APPLIED:

1. SENTENCE RHYTHM
   - Original: Longer, flowing sentences
   - Lehane: Short, punchy sentences for impact ("Just silence." "She mattered.")
   - More periods. More white space. More impact.

2. VISUAL PUNCTUATION
   - Original: Commas connecting thoughts
   - Lehane: Periods creating pauses. Fragments for emphasis.
   - "Tap. Tap. Tap." vs. "tapped against his case"

3. SHOWING vs. TELLING
   - Original: More explanation, more context
   - Lehane: More action, less explanation
   - Physical reactions emphasized (sweating palms, jaw tightening)

4. SILENCE/UNSPOKEN
   - Original: States what's happening
   - Lehane: Shows what's NOT being said
   - The silence between Nick and Jackie becomes a character

5. LOSS OF INNOCENCE
   - Original: Describes the moment
   - Lehane: Shows the precise moment of fracture
   - "And something broke." - Short. Powerful. Final.

6. PHYSICAL DETAILS
   - Original: Good physical details
   - Lehane: Physical details tied more directly to emotion
   - Bodies carry emotion, not just thoughts

7. DIALOGUE
   - Original: Natural dialogue
   - Lehane: More subtext, more pauses, more what's not said
   - Jackie's careful speech more emphasized
