## New Details from Timothy (April 2026)

### Factual / Historical Details

- **WBCN brought back DJs** for a real "celebration of closure." Maxanne draws the short straw and gets the final shift no one wanted. DJs conflicted about "celebrating" their home closing.
- **Final planned song** was Pink Floyd. Maxanne holds the Pink Floyd LP, then puts it down and plays Nick/Jackie's demo tape instead. This is the climax — choosing two unknown kids over Pink Floyd.
- **Bruce Springsteen** was interviewed by Maxanne live on WBCN in 1973 before he was famous. Bruce said, "This is my first time on the radio, my mom lives out in California, HI MOM!" Reference this in Maxanne's backstory to establish her credibility as talent-spotter.
- **Camp name:** Camp Elbanobscot (NOT Winnipesaukee). Real Sudbury music camp. Music teachers from all over. Breeding ground for young musicians.
- **PHISH** came up through Lincoln-Sudbury area in late 70s/early 80s. Reference briefly in Maxanne's narration as atmosphere, not plot.
- **The Neighbors** — real band from neighboring town, won battle at The Rathskeller ("The Rat") in Boston. Nick and Jackie are symbolic of this energy — "the best band that never was."

### Character Details

- **Grace Johnson** works her way up to Manager at Friendly's. This is seen as a success for her. Play up the irony that at "Friendly's" the people in town are not so "friendly" unless you're in the same class. Subtle, not heavy-handed.
- **Patty Lally** hums "Go Your Own Way" (Fleetwood Mac) under her breath as a habit — not as a statement but as something she does without thinking. Other characters hear it. By the time she performs it on stage, the reader already knows the melody. It's been planted.
- **Dr. Marsh's confession** about giving up guitar is the mirror image of Dan's secret guitar playing. One hid the dream, one invested in it. Same regret, different class expression.

### Places as Characters

These locations should have sensory personality that recurs and evolves:

- **Hop Brook:** Sound of water over stones. Different in spring (loud, rushing) vs summer (quiet, almost gone). The wooden bridge that groans.
- **The Dump:** Saturday morning democracy. The smell. The bulldozers. Every family's garbage looking the same.
- **Friendly's:** Fryer oil. Polyester uniform. Name tag font. Coffee refills. The irony of the name.
- **Mini-bike trails:** Two-stroke exhaust. Angry hornet engine sound. Mud on everything. Freedom of speed when you own nothing else.
- **Camp Elbanobscot:** Pine sap. Lake water. Music drifting from cabins. The outdoor camp that "for some odd reason specializes in music."
- **The Garage:** Sawdust and motor oil. Dan's tools on pegboards. Concrete floor. The milk crates.

### Style Notes

- **Show don't tell** is Timothy's #1 challenge. Professor K's #1 focus.
- **Chapter titles as rock songs** — save for final polish pass. Each pre-1980 song emotionally matches chapter content.
- **Novel title:** Keep "Walk This Way." It's an invitation, not a description.
- **The story is based on real life** but is fiction now. Real details ground it; fiction frees it.
- **Timothy's #1 editorial weakness:** Not trusting the reader. Over-explaining what the audience already knows. Cut every sentence that tells the reader what they just witnessed.
