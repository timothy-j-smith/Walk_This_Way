## Real Boston Rock History (Weave In)

### WBCN Facts

- WBCN 104.1 FM — "The Rock of Boston" — 56 years on air
- Final broadcast: August 11, 2009 (became Christian contemporary)
- Maxanne Sartori: discovered Aerosmith in a bar (1972–73), broke them on WBCN
- Hosted Bruce Springsteen in 1973 before fame
- John Lennon warned about Springsteen's impact — Maxanne can reference this once
- The station that broke Aerosmith, The Cars, J. Geils Band

### Boston Venues

- **The Rat** (Kenmore Square): Legendary dive. Sacred ground. Now luxury condos ($2.2M for 2,400 sq ft). Joey Ramone ducked under pipes backstage. The Ramones played there in '77.
- **The Neighborhoods**: Real Boston band. One reference in Maxanne 2009 narration.
- **Lansdowne Street**: Could buy the whole block for what one condo costs now.

### Period Details (1979)

- **Store 24**: Convenience chain. Both boys shop there. Period-authentic.
- Disco dying — Disco Demolition Night July 12, 1979, Comiskey Park.
- The Clash about to teach America punk had something to say.
- MTV three years away. Image hasn't replaced sound yet.
- Last moment when talent + passion could matter more than marketing.

### Music Details

- Aerosmith: "Dream On" (1973), "Sweet Emotion," "Walk This Way"
- Queen: "Bohemian Rhapsody," "Somebody to Love," "Under Pressure"
- Fleetwood Mac: "Dreams," "Go Your Own Way," "Landslide," "The Chain"
- Dave Brubeck: Dan's jazz influence on Nick (garage record collection)

## The Ultimate Scalpel & Hose Test

For every sentence, every scene, every chapter:

1. **SCALPEL**: Is this earned? If you showed it, cut the explanation. If it's purple, ground it. If it's an adverb, kill it. If it's passive, activate it.

2. **HOSE**: Is this specific? Can I smell it, hear it, feel it? Does the dialogue sound like this character and no other? Is there a money sentence within reach?

3. **THE HONOR TEST**: Does this honor Nick? Does this reach Jackie? Would the author be proud to read this aloud to them?

If any answer is no, fix it or cut it.

## Prose DNA Targets

- **Voice**: Lehane's directness + Russo's compassion + King's accessibility
- **Sentences**: Short. Medium with character. Longer reveal. (The Lehane pattern.)
- **Details**: What people smell like. What's in their fridge. How they pronounce words. What they can't afford.
- **Emotion**: Through objects, actions, silence. Never named. Never explained.
- **Music**: Specific songs. Technical details. Physical sensation. Listener reaction. Never "soared" or "transcendent."
