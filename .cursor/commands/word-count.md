# Word Count Tracker
## Track Your Writing Progress

**Command:** `/word-count` or `/wc` or `/words`

**Action:** Shows word count for your novel and logs it.

**Usage:** Type `/word-count` in Cursor Chat to get current word count.

**What It Shows:**
- Total words in draft
- Date logged
- Progress tracking

**Script:** `.cursor/scripts/word_count.sh`
