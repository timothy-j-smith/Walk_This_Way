=======================================================================
HOW TO READ THIS OUTLINE
=======================================================================

Each chapter has:
- A TITLE (working title -- can change)
- A POV (whose eyes we see through)
- A TIMELINE (when it takes place)
- A PURPOSE (what this chapter does for the novel)
- BEATS (the 4-6 key moments in the chapter, in order)
- SOURCE (what existing material feeds this chapter)

Think of beats like stepping stones across a river. You don't need
to know every word before you start. You just need to know where
to put your foot next.

A BEAT is not a full scene. It's a moment, an image, a turn. 
Examples of beats from what you've already written:
- "Thomas lifts his head from the sand" (a beat)
- "Rashford's voice goes warm, then flat again" (a beat)
- "Mirko casts his line. Seaweed again." (a beat)
- "Sally smiled." (a beat)

You already write in beats. You just didn't know the word for it.

=======================================================================
ABOUT THE TOOTHPASTE FACTORY INTERLUDES
=======================================================================

Between most chapters, a short stream-of-consciousness passage.
Thomas's coma-mind. No punctuation, no capitals, no rules.

These are labeled as INTERLUDE in the outline.

Act 1 interludes are SHORT -- 1-3 paragraphs. Fragmented. Confused.
Thomas's mind is still waking up. As the novel progresses (Acts 2
and 3), they get longer, clearer, angrier, more alive.

You already wrote the first interlude. It's the Toothpaste Factory
passage. We'll use it and write more like it.
