## Generate Prose from Scene Outline
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Testing  
**Integration:** COMPASS DNA + Smart Rewrites System

---

## 🎯 WHAT THIS DOES

**Generates complete first-draft prose from your scene outline**, using:
- Character DNA (for voice consistency)
- Plot DNA (for story coherence)
- World DNA (for setting accuracy)

**Then you refine it** before feeding into Smart Rewrites for enhancement.

---

## 💡 TIPS

1. **Be specific in outline** - More detail = better first draft
2. **Use Character DNA** - System automatically uses character voices
3. **Refine everything** - Generated draft is starting point, not final
4. **Then use Smart Rewrites** - Enhance after you've added your voice
5. **It's optional** - Use when helpful, skip when you prefer writing from scratch
