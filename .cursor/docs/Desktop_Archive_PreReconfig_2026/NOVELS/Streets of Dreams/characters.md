# Character Profiles: The Last Football Match

## Main Characters

### Youssef Benali (Protagonist)
- **Age**: 11-12 years old
- **Background**: Lives in medina above spice merchant's shop
- **Personality**: Determined, imaginative, learns from observation
- **Football Style**: Feline grace, unpredictable movement, street-smart
- **Dream**: Play for Morocco in 2030 World Cup
- **Nickname**: "Qit siḥrī" (Magic Cat)
- **Key Traits**: Absorbs lessons from cats, uses shadows as opponents

### Omar (Best Friend)
- **Role**: Youssef's closest friend and teammate
- **Personality**: Supportive, strategic thinker
- **Function**: Provides iPhone access through brother Khalid
- **Development**: Emerges as team strategist during preparation

### Ahmed Benali (Father)
- **Background**: Construction worker, former street footballer
- **Personality**: Proud but realistic about dreams
- **Role**: Provides emotional support and family history
- **Quote**: "The world has a way of making you forget your dreams if you're not careful"

### Fatima Benali (Mother)
- **Role**: Caring mother, prepares meals, supports family
- **Personality**: Warm, concerned about family's future
- **Function**: Grounds Youssef in family reality

### Aicha (Little Sister)
- **Age**: 8 years old
- **Role**: Represents family innocence and future
- **Function**: Shows family dynamics and Youssef's protective nature

## Supporting Characters

### Khalid (Omar's Brother)
- **Role**: Provides iPhone access for video analysis
- **Function**: Gateway to modern football knowledge
- **Deal**: Mathematics homework in exchange for video access

### The Cats (Metaphorical Teachers)
- **Hamza**: Scarred orange tabby - deliberate confidence, positioning
- **Lalla**: Sleek black female - patience then explosive action
- **Simo**: Small gray tom - anticipation, spatial intelligence

### Academy Players
- **Badr**: Academy captain, gracious in defeat
- **Team**: Represent privilege and formal training

### Beach Players
- **Karim**: Leader of Anfa beach players, graceful dancer-like movement
- **Team**: Bridge between street and academy styles

## Character Arcs

### Youssef's Journey
1. **Street Player** → **Video Student** → **Tactical Thinker** → **National Prospect**
2. **Individual Skill** → **Team Leadership** → **Community Hero**

### Family Dynamics
- **Ahmed**: Past dreams → Present reality → Future hope through son
- **Fatima**: Concern → Support → Pride
- **Community**: Skeptical → Supportive → Celebratory
