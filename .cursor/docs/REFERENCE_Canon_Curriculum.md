## Three-Read Protocol

From HANK research. Same spirit as Professor **RE** skill — turned into a reading habit.

### Read 1 — Experience

Just read. Feel. Enjoy. Be moved. No notes required.

### Read 2 — Analysis

Stop every chapter (or every scene). Ask:

- What just happened?
- Why this scene length?
- Why this information **now**?
- How does this advance character, plot, or theme?

### Read 3 — Craft Study

Pick **one element only** for the whole book:

- Dialogue
- Structure / time
- POV / reliability
- Image / sense
- Ending as argument

Track that element across the entire work. See the pattern.

**Invoke in chat:** “Three-read prep for *[book]* — I’m on Read 2, tracking [element].”

## HANK Synthesis — Quick Reference

**Seven universal truths (headlines):**

1. Suffering shapes us — meaning is chosen
2. Love requires vulnerability
3. Identity is negotiated (verb, not noun)
4. History haunts — past isn’t past
5. Power corrupts through language
6. Meaning emerges from story
7. Death clarifies what mattered

**Five craft patterns:**

1. Structure embodies meaning (form = argument)
2. Voice as destiny
3. Time as material
4. Metaphor as structure (not decoration)
5. Ending as argument

**One line to keep:**

> What do I see that needs to be shown — and what form does that truth require?

Same question as spine finding, frame-vs-brick, and Dickey’s “record fear, don’t explain it.”
