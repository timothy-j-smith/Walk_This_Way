# CHARACTER VOICE DNA — WALK THIS WAY

**File Purpose:** Quick-reference voice DNA for the Writing DNA Memory System and SmartWrite passes. Use this when calibrating tone, diction, rhythm, and emotional subtext.

---

## Nick Johnson (Age 15-17, 1976-1979)

- **Baseline Rhythm:** Short, muscular sentences; clipped replies when guarded; longer lines only when talking about music.
- **Diction:** Working-class Boston teen. Uses contractions, plain verbs, and sensory detail tied to tools, grease, weather.
- **Emotional Tell:** Tight chest, jaw set, fingers on guitar strings. Tension shows before he speaks.
- **Mode Switches:**
  - **Calm/Focused:** Practical, direct (“We need to fix the carb first.”)
  - **Defensive:** Sarcastic edge, rhetorical questions (“Why do you care what they think?”)
  - **Inspired (music):** Vivid metaphors tied to engines, amps, thunder.
- **Red Flags:** Fancy vocabulary, long academic constructions, corporate metaphors.

---

## Jackie Marsh (Age 15-17, 1976-1979)

- **Baseline Rhythm:** Balanced clauses, intentional pauses; defaults to complete sentences. When anxious, removes contractions.
- **Diction:** Educated, precise. References composers, theory, polished imagery (light, architecture, composed gestures).
- **Emotional Tell:** Adjusts posture, smooths sleeves, glances to gauge reactions. Internal monologue analyzes before reacting.
- **Mode Switches:**
  - **Confident/Performing:** Warm, persuasive, theatrical language.
  - **Conflicted:** Sentences lengthen; inserts self-corrections (“I mean—what I am trying to say—”).
  - **With Nick Alone:** Slightly looser, shows hidden humor, still more formal than Nick.
- **Red Flags:** Slang-heavy dialogue, dropped grammar, blue-collar idioms.

---

## Patty Lally (Age 15-17, 1976-1979)

- **Baseline Rhythm:** Calm, empathetic, grounded. Uses sensory anchors (weather, sound) to summarize emotional states.
- **Diction:** Plainspoken but observant. Feels like thoughtful journal entries or frequency reports.
- **Emotional Tell:** Rarely dramatizes; instead reflects and synthesizes (“It sounded like you were both playing defense.”).
- **Role:** Frequency keeper. Letters identify imbalances and invite honesty without judgment.
- **Red Flags:** Overly poetic turns, harsh judgment, detachment from emotional reality.

---

## Maxanne Sartori (Age 73, 2024 Frame Narrative)

- **Baseline Rhythm:** Radio cadence—conversational, intimate, uses beats and ellipses like a live broadcast.
- **Diction:** 1970s/80s Boston radio lexicon. References vinyl, turntables, cart machines, sponsors, FCC rules.
- **Emotional Tell:** Nostalgia with a bitter edge; laughs at herself, cuts to truth quickly.
- **Mode Switches:**
  - **On-Air Professional:** Warm, cool authority.
  - **Off-Script Memory:** Drops into storytelling, cups the past like a vinyl jacket.
- **Red Flags:** Modern tech slang, corporate buzzwords, generic narrator tone.

## Voice Interaction Guidance

- **Nick ↔ Jackie:** Tension lives in contrast. Keep volleying rhythms—Nick short/physical, Jackie composed/formal.
- **Nick ↔ Patty:** Patty disarms. Nick’s sentences lengthen slightly, but slang stays.
- **Jackie ↔ Patty:** Patty encourages Jackie to speak plainly; monitor contraction usage for emotional truth.
- **Maxanne ↔ 1970s Scenes:** Maxanne frames events with specific station detail, never general nostalgia.
