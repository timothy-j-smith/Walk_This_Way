# GitKraken Pro Preferences - Quick Guide
**Created:** January 22, 2026  
**Where to find Preferences on Mac**

---

## 🎯 WHERE TO FIND PREFERENCES

**On Mac, Preferences is NOT under File menu!**

### **Method 1: Keyboard Shortcut (Fastest)**
- Press: `Cmd+,` (Command + Comma)
- This opens Preferences directly!

### **Method 2: Application Menu**
1. **Look at the top menu bar**
2. **Click "GitKraken"** (the menu next to the Apple logo 🍎)
3. **Click "Preferences..."**

**The menu order is usually:**
- 🍎 Apple
- **GitKraken** ← Click this!
- File
- Edit
- View
- etc.

---

## ✅ ONCE IN PREFERENCES

1. **Left sidebar:** Click "Authentication"
2. **GitHub section:** 
   - Should show your connected account
   - Or click "Connect to GitHub" / "Add"
   - Enter: Username `timothy-j-smith`
   - Token: Your new token (no expiration)

---

## 💡 QUICK CHECK

**If you can't find it:**
- Try `Cmd+,` keyboard shortcut
- Or look for "Settings" instead of "Preferences" (some versions use different names)
