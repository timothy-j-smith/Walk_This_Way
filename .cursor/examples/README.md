## HOW TO USE

### Creating Examples:
1. Find your best writing sample
2. Create Good/Bad pair:
   - **Good:** Your best writing that works
   - **Bad:** Similar writing that doesn't work (or generic version)
3. Save to appropriate directory
4. Reference in prompts using Few-Shot Examples template

### Example Format:
```markdown
# Nick's Voice - Working-Class Authenticity

## GOOD EXAMPLE:
"I didn't need his help. I could handle it. My old man taught me that much before he died."

## BAD EXAMPLE:
"I did not require his assistance. I was capable of managing the situation independently. My father had instilled this self-reliance in me prior to his passing."

## WHY IT WORKS:
- Short, direct sentences
- Working-class dialect ("didn't need" vs "did not require")
- Emotional subtext (defensive pride)
- Authentic voice
```
