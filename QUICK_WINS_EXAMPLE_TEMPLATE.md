```

### Example Filled-In (For "Walk This Way"):

## 🎯 STEP 3: DETAILED SCENE REWRITE TEMPLATE

### Template (Copy This):
```
<scene>
<characters></characters>
<goal></goal>
<tone></tone>
<constraint></constraint>
<technique></technique>
</scene>

Rewrite this scene:
[your scene text]
```

### Example Filled-In (For "Walk This Way"):
```
<scene>
<characters>Nick, Jackie</characters>
<goal>Show Nick pushing Jackie away after his father's death</goal>
<tone>Raw anger masking grief, defensive pride</tone>
<constraint>No exposition, show through Nick's actions and short dialogue</constraint>
<technique>Use short, punchy sentences for Nick's anger, longer sentences for Jackie's concern</technique>
</scene>

Rewrite this scene:
[Your existing scene text here]
```

## 🧠 STEP 4: CHAIN OF THOUGHT TEMPLATE

### Template (Copy This):
```
<thinking>
- What does [character] want?
- What's the subtext?
- What are the consequences?
</thinking>

[Your request]
```

### Example Filled-In (For "Walk This Way"):
```
<thinking>
- What does Nick want? To be left alone, to prove he can handle it, to not show weakness
- What's the subtext? He's terrified of being vulnerable, he's drowning in grief, he's pushing away the one person who understands
- What are the consequences? This moment breaks their friendship irreparably, sets up the class divide theme
</thinking>

Write the scene where Nick rejects Jackie's help after Dan's death.
```

<goal></goal>
<tone></tone>
<constraint></constraint>
</scene>

Analyze and provide fixes.
```

### Example Filled-In (For "Walk This Way"):
```
You are a line editor specializing in working-class authenticity.

<thinking>
- What does Nick want? To prove he doesn't need help, to maintain his pride
- What's the subtext? He's drowning in grief but can't show it, class differences make accepting help feel like weakness
- What are the consequences? This moment destroys their friendship, sets up the rest of the novel's class divide theme
</thinking>

<scene>
<goal>Show Nick's defensive rejection of Jackie's help</goal>
<tone>Raw anger masking deep vulnerability</tone>
<constraint>No exposition, show through action and dialogue, maintain working-class authenticity</constraint>
</scene>

Analyze this scene and provide specific fixes to make it more powerful.
```

## 💡 HOW TO USE THESE EXAMPLES

### Step 1: Copy the Template
- Go to `.cursor/rules/quick-wins-templates.md`
- Find the template you want
- Copy it

### Step 2: Fill in the Brackets
- Replace `[character]` with actual character names (Nick, Jackie, etc.)
- Replace `[goal]` with your specific goal
- Replace `[tone]` with the emotional quality you want
- Replace `[constraint]` with what NOT to do

### Step 3: Paste into Cursor
- Open Cursor Chat, Agent, or Cmd+K
- Paste your filled-in template
- Add your scene text or request
- Send!
