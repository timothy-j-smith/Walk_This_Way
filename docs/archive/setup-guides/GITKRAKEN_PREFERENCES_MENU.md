**Created:** January 22, 2026  
**Problem:** Cmd+, just zooms, doesn't open Preferences

## ✅ USE THE MENU BAR (EASIEST)

**Since Cmd+, isn't working, use the menu:**

1. **Look at the top menu bar** (at the very top of your screen)
2. **Click "GitKraken"** (the first menu, next to the Apple logo 🍎)
3. **Look for:** "Preferences..." or "Settings..."
4. **Click it**

**The menu should show:**
- About GitKraken
- Check for Updates
- **Preferences...** ← This is what you want!
- (other options)

## 🎯 ALTERNATIVE: PROFILE ICON

**I see in your screenshot there's a "Default Profile" icon in the top right:**

2. **Look for:** "Settings" or "Preferences" or "Account Settings"
