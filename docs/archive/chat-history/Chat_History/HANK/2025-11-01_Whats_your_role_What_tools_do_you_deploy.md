## Why This Prose Works:

1. Visual/metaphorical specificity: The guitar held together with "electrical tape and pure willpower" immediately shows class, determination, and limitation.

2. Emotional subtext: "finding notes that shouldn't have been possible" suggests struggle, impossibility, and defiance—not just talent.

3. The tools passage layers meaning:
   - "Shrine to honest work" = ritualized respect
   - "Missing the 9/16ths he'd had in his pocket that day" = precise, personal detail that anchors loss
   - "The weight of responsibility" = class and generational weight, not just sadness

4. Musical metaphor as character: "extension of his soul, a way to channel everything he couldn't say with words" ties Nick’s music to his emotional need—expression as survival.

5. Theme integration: The tools-to-music tension embodies the working-class themes—honest labor vs. dreams, inheritance vs. escape.

This connects character (Nick), setting (the garage), theme (class/authenticity), and emotion (loss/responsibility). It uses concrete detail to reveal interior and social context.
