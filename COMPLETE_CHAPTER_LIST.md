### CHAPTER 23: THE BATTLE OF THE BANDS - PART I (Bands 1-4)

### CHAPTER 24: THE BATTLE OF THE BANDS - PART II (Bands 5-7)

### CHAPTER 25: THE BATTLE OF THE BANDS - PART III (Results & Aftermath)
**Timeline:** March 15, 1979 (10:00-11:30 PM)  
**POV:** Maxanne Sartori (First Person, witnessing) + Third Person (Nick/Jackie)  
**Location:** Lincoln-Sudbury High School cafeteria + parking lot  
**Word Count Target:** 3,000-3,500 words

**Key Elements:**

**Band 8: The Throwbacks (Comedy band)**
- Quick, funny, crowd relief after intensity
- Not serious contenders but needed palette cleanser

**Judges Deliberation:**
- Maxanne: Walking the Line and Rumours tied for authenticity
- Other judges: Crown Jewels perfect, Walking the Line too raw
- 30-minute debate
- Maxanne: "Walking the Line played truth"
- Final vote: Walking the Line wins (Maxanne's vote decides it)

**Award Ceremony:**
- Nick accepts: dedicates to his father
- Tries to share moment with Jackie
- Reaches out to Jackie on stage
- Jackie frozen, Dr. Marsh intervening from audience
- "We need to go, NOW. Juilliard auditions tomorrow."
- Jackie torn: looks at Nick's hand, looks at father
- Chooses father, walks away
- Nick's face: betrayal complete
- Patty tries to get to both of them, crowd blocking

**Parking Lot:**
- Class divisions immediately reassert
- Working-class kids celebrating with Nick
- Upper-class kids comforting Jackie
- Dr. Marsh: "You played well until that song. Never play that again."
- Jackie: silent, broken
- Nick sees Jackie leaving, their eyes meet
- Everything unsaid
- Grace finds Nick: "Let's go home, honey"
