## 📝 **PROSE ENHANCEMENT STRATEGY**

### **Voice Consistency**
- **Maxanne's narration** - Conversational, nostalgic, authentic
- **Nick's dialogue** - Working-class directness
- **Jackie's dialogue** - Educated but conflicted
- **Period authenticity** - 1970s language and references

### **Show Don't Tell**
- **Emotions** - Physical reactions, actions
- **Class differences** - Concrete details, not abstract
- **Musical talent** - Specific techniques, not general praise
- **Setting** - Sensory details, not descriptions

### **Sensory Details**
- **Sound** - Music, equipment, voices
- **Smell** - Motor oil, bourbon, vinyl
- **Touch** - Guitar strings, piano keys, equipment
- **Sight** - Visual details of 1970s Boston
- **Taste** - Period-appropriate food and drink

### **Sentence Variety**
- **Short sentences** - For tension, action
- **Long sentences** - For reflection, description
- **Dialogue tags** - Varied and specific
- **Paragraph structure** - Rhythm and flow
