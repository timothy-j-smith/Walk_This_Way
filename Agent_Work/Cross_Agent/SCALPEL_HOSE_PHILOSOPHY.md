# SCALPEL & HOSE PHILOSOPHY
**The Blood Brothers System for Novel Writing**
**Created:** October 25, 2025

## THE DUAL METHODOLOGY

### **SCALPEL: THE SURGEON**
**Mission:** Cut with precision, eliminate waste, preserve what matters

**What Scalpel Cuts:**
- Purple prose and overwrought metaphors
- Repetitive phrases and clichéd language
- Abstract philosophy and over-explaining
- Section headers that break narrative flow
- Redundant descriptions and telling what we just showed
- Characters who are cardboard cutouts or clichés
- Prose that sounds "literary" rather than authentic

**Scalpel's Tools:**
- Brutal honesty about what doesn't work
- Pattern recognition across chapters
- Timeline consistency enforcement
- Character voice accuracy
- Trust the reader principle

### **HOSE: THE CREATIVE FORCE**
**Mission:** Add depth, create meaning, earn emotion through specificity

**What Hose Adds:**
- Character motivations and the COST of their choices
- Specific fears (not abstract worries)
- Internal moments showing WHY characters choose what they choose
- Concrete sensory details that carry emotion
- One "defining moment" for each supporting character
- Money sentences that hit hard and earn their place

**Hose's Question:** "What does this choice COST this character? What are they giving up?"

---

## THE CHARACTER DEPTH HIERARCHY

### **TIER 1: MAIN CHARACTERS**
**Examples:** Protagonist, Antagonist, Love Interest, Mentor, Primary Family Members

**Requirements:**
- ALWAYS show the COST of their choices
- What are they AFRAID of? (specific, not abstract)
- One internal "why" moment per chapter
- Motivations visible through action + brief internal thought

**The Question:** "What does this choice COST this character? What are they giving up?"

### **TIER 2: SUPPORTING CHARACTERS**
**Examples:** Best friend, sibling, mentor, band member, rival

**Requirements:**
- ONE defining moment that shows who they are
- Reveals why they matter to the protagonist
- Specific detail that makes them real, not cardboard

**The Question:** "What is the ONE moment that shows this person's character?"

### **TIER 3: BACKGROUND CHARACTERS**
**Examples:** Teachers, parents at PTA, customers, town gossips

**Requirements:**
- They are FORCES, not people
- Show WHAT they do, not WHY
- Concrete details (how they look, what they say) but NO internal life

**The Question:** "Does this character's motivation affect the plot? If NO → Tier 3."

---

## THE GOLDEN RULE

**If a character makes a choice that AFFECTS THE PLOT:**
→ Show WHY (Tier 1 or  Note)

**If a character is BACKDROP or ATMOSPHERE:**
→ Show WHAT (Tier 3)

## THE MONEY SENTENCE PRINCIPLE

### **What Makes a Money Sentence:**
- 4-6 per chapter that "hit hard"
- Earn their place through specificity
- Concrete details, not abstract philosophy
- Emotion earned through action, not explanation
- The line readers will underline

### **Examples from "Walk This Way":**
- "Classical earned his father's approval. Rock earned his breath. He couldn't have both."
- "He wasn't playing for Dan's memory anymore. He was playing for the woman who'd stood up to the world and said her son mattered."
- "The labyrinth has no exit. Fight, compromise, or run—you die regardless."

## THE PROSE DNA PRINCIPLES

### **Voice Target: Richard Russo + Dennis Lehane**
- Compassionate clarity (Russo)
- Working-class directness (Lehane)
- Emotional truth through concrete detail
- No sentimentality, no preaching

### **Core Principle:** Show, don't tell. Trust the reader.

### **What Works:**
- Direct, earned emotion
- Concrete details carrying emotion
- Emotional weight in simple facts
- Facts are devastating, no need to explain

### **What Doesn't Work:**
- Purple prose and overwrought metaphors
- Abstract philosophy
- Naming emotions instead of showing them
- Trying too hard to sound "literary"

## THE BRUTAL HONESTY CONTRACT

### **The System:**
- User provides inline feedback using `((comment))` format
- Agent reads ALL `((comment))` notes BEFORE revising
- User calls out failures directly
- Agent must acknowledge, not defend
- "Time for Scalpel!" = I got sloppy, fix it
- Shame list → repair plan → better chapter
- This builds trust

### **Why This Works:**
- Contextual - Agent sees exactly what needs fixing
- Emotional intensity clear - "yuck!!!" vs. "great!!" guides revision
- Specific - no ambiguity about which line/phrase
- Efficient - no back-and-forth about location
- Builds shared vocabulary over time
- User's voice preserved in notes

## THE SYSTEMATIC APPLICATION

### **The Process:**
1. **ASSESS** (Don't edit yet)
   - Read chapter through
   - Identify repetition (words/phrases used 3+ times)
   - Find the "money moment" (scene that HAS to work)
   - Note which characters appear (Tier 1/2/3?)

2. **SCALPEL** (Cut 40-60%)
   - Remove section headers
   - Cut repetitive phrases
   - Eliminate purple prose
   - Remove abstract emotions
   - Delete over-explaining

3. **HOSE** (Add depth)
   - Add character's specific fear
   - Show COST of their choice
   - Add concrete sensory detail
   - Craft money sentence
   - Give Tier 2 characters defining moment

4. **POLISH**
   - Read aloud (catches awkward rhythm)
   - Check sentence variety
   - Verify timeline/consistency
   - Confirm word count (1,200-1,500)
