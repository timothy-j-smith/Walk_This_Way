### **BEAT 2: JACKIE & CROWN JEWELS (400 words)**
**Location:** Lincoln-Sudbury Music Room
**Time:** Same afternoon, May 14th, 1979

**Scene Setup:**
- Jackie and his band (Elizabeth Marsh, Ace Callahan, Max Wesslen) running through their set
- **Queen Songs:** "Bohemian Rhapsody," "Somebody to Love," "We Will Rock You," "We Are the Champions"
- Proper acoustics, professional equipment, music stands
- Band finishes rehearsal, packs up gear
- Jackie stays behind to practice alone ((Dr. Marsh-Jackie-Queen/Rock)) ((He doesn't want to die on the insides))

**Jackie's Internal Moment:**
- **Specific Fear:** "What if I never earn Dad's approval, no matter what I achieve?"
- **Cost of Choice:** Carrying the weight of representing privileged excellence
- **Internal Conflict:** Technical perfection vs. genuine emotion
- **Money Sentence:** About classical vs. rock, disgust vs. breath

**THE ONE CRITICAL THING TO SHOW:** The crack in the armor - the slight imperfection in his perfect world that he's blind to, but we can see. He's so caught up in pleasing others and calculating how to do it all that he can't see the forest through the trees.

**Ending:** Jackie alone at the Steinway, practicing "Bohemian Rhapsody"

### **BEAT 3: PATTY & RUMOURS (400 words)**
**Location:** St. Michael's Church, Concord
**Time:** Same afternoon, May 14th, 1979

**Scene Setup:**
- Patty and her band (Robin Bosshard and others) working on their set
- **Fleetwood Mac Songs:** "Dreams," "Go Your Own Way," "Rhiannon"
- Simple instruments, echoing acoustics of the church
- Band finishes rehearsal, packs up gear
- Patty stays behind to practice alone

**Patty's Internal Moment:**
- **Specific Fear:** "What if I can't remind them what they're missing?"
- **Cost of Choice:** Being the messenger of loss of innocence
- **Internal Conflict:** Rising above vs. being sucked into the vortex
- **Money Sentence:** About her freedom vs. their traps

**THE ONE CRITICAL THING TO SHOW:** Her resignation - she's already accepted that she can't save them, but she's going to try anyway. Her loss of innocence is different from the boys - they're losing it through external pressure, but she's losing it through witnessing their loss. ((Boys - Patty - Harmony))((chose her path))
