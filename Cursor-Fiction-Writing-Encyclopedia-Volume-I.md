### 7. FILE TYPE DECISIONS: .txt vs .md

**When to Use .txt Files:**
- ✅ **Novel chapters** (your story content)
- ✅ **Character profiles** (simple text descriptions)
- ✅ **Research notes** (plain text information)
- ✅ **Simple documentation** (basic instructions)
- ✅ **Clean, distraction-free** writing environment

**When to Use .md Files:**
- ✅ **Commands and rules** (need headers, lists, formatting)
- ✅ **Documentation** (setup instructions, guides)
- ✅ **Templates** (structured content with formatting)
- ✅ **Reference materials** (organized information)
- ✅ **Files with structure** (headers, bullet points, emphasis)

**Decision Rule:**
- **Story content = .txt** (clean, simple, focused on writing)
- **System files = .md** (structured, formatted, organized)

**Examples:**

**Use .txt for:**
```
Ch_01 - Walk This Way TXT.txt
Maxanne-Character-Profile.txt
Boston-1970s-Research.txt
```

**Use .md for:**
```
prose.md (command file)
character-rules.md (rule file)
Universal-Fiction-Templates.md (template file)
```
